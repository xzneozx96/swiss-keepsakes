# Design system review 01: HeroUI v3 fidelity

Reviewed: `docs/ux/design-system/project/` (README.md, tokens.json, 48 cards in `components/`), on 2026-10-13.

Sources:
- **CSS:** `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/heroui-inc/heroui/v3/packages/styles/components/<file>`. Every component file cited below was fetched from this location for this review, together with `utilities/index.css`.
- **Variables and theme:** `docs/ux/heroui-reference/source-variables.css` and `source-theme.css`.
- **Spec files:** "ref 0X §n" means a section of `docs/ux/heroui-reference/0X-*.md`.
- **Principles:** "principles §1" means https://www.heroui.com/docs/react/getting-started/design-principles: secondary = alternative actions, tertiary = dismissive (cancel, skip, use sparingly), danger = destructive.
- **Component list:** the 72 component docs pages under `apps/docs/content/docs/en/react/components` (branch `v3`), as listed by the GitHub API.

Card paths are relative to `docs/ux/design-system/project/components/`.

## Verdict

**No blockers, 8 majors, 16 minors, 8 nits.**

The system is a close rebuild of HeroUI v3 geometry. All of these match the CSS:
- Button and toggle heights, paddings and pill radii.
- Radii: 24px for cards, popovers and dialogs; 12px for fields; 16px for items.
- Calendar and RangeCalendar metrics.
- Dialog, drawer and toast spacing.
- Chip, Tag and Badge sizes.
- Most state descriptions.

There are no blockers. The majors fall into two clusters:

1. **The agreed visible field border is specified as a colour only, and its knock-on effects are not worked through.** There is no width token, so HeroUI's `--field-border-width: 0px` would hide the border entirely. Once the width is set to 1px, HeroUI's CSS draws dividers in InputGroup and borders around the NumberField steppers. Padding-sized fields grow to 42px, and the field shadow is dropped or replaced. None of this appears on the cards.
2. **Several specimens misuse HeroUI's semantic variants or invent shapes:**
   - Non-dismissive actions are drawn as tertiary while the READMEs call them "secondary".
   - The ButtonGroup card uses a ButtonGroup for single selection.
   - The ScrollShadow card shows an ink-filled chip rail that no HeroUI component produces.
   - Cancel and refund have two contradictory homes.

Most "Our changes" entries are labelled and justified. Eight departures are undocumented, two entries are mislabelled, and one state is missing (Checkbox and Radio hover); see the audit below. Coverage is complete: the 72 docs pages plus EmptyState and Header all map onto the 47 component cards.

## What is right

- **Button family:** 40/36 heights, 16px padding, 24px pills and icon sizes; press scale .97/.98/.96; disabled at 50%. All seven variants are drawn, and their colours match button.css.
- **Shape scale:** 24px cards, popovers, dialogs, toasts and alerts; 12px fields and tooltips; 16px list items, chips and drawer top corners. The 20px Tabs list, Table root and DatePicker/ColorPicker popovers are correctly told apart from the 24px elsewhere (date-picker.css and tabs.css use `--radius * 2.5`).
- **Calendar and RangeCalendar:** 252px root, 36px circular cells, 40px row pitch, and the band rounding (24px at range ends, 8px at week wraps) all match. Today, selected, unavailable and disabled (struck) states are drawn as defined.
- **Overlays:**
  - Modal and AlertDialog: 24px padding and 8px/20px section gaps; close trigger 16px from the top and end; 40px icon chip. AlertDialog is drawn non-dismissible, with a tertiary "Behalten" next to a danger button.
  - Drawer: 16px top radius, 85vh cap and 36×4 handle.
- **Chip, Tag and Badge:** size tables match chip.css, tag.css and badge.css (20/24/28, 20/24/32 and 16/28/32).
- **Tabs:** primary separators hide beside the selected tab, exactly as tabs.css does.
- **Field text:** 16px field text below 640px is respected everywhere.
- **Variant use:** one primary per context, and solid danger only on a destructive confirm.
- **Departure labelling:** most departures are labelled where they apply. Read-only fields, safe-area padding and touch-target padding cover gaps that HeroUI itself documents.
