# 04 · Visual craft, consistency and handoff

Reviewed 13.10.2026. Scope: the 49 preview cards in `docs/ux/design-system/project/components/` (48 components + Cover), the root `README.md`, `tokens.json` and each card's `README.md`. Rendered from the review harness at `http://127.0.0.1:8765/` (`tokens.css` with `bundle.css` appended, same as the published card). Screenshots: `.playwright-mcp/review/desktop/` (1280px full page, plus `<Comp>-card-<W>x<H>.png` at the declared card size) and `.playwright-mcp/review/m375/` (375px full page). All measurements come from `getBoundingClientRect` in Chromium unless stated otherwise.

I did not open the published artifact. Findings 2 (clipping) and 6 (bottom bands) assume the card frame is fixed at the `@dsCard` `height=`, as that metadata implies. If the artifact sizes cards to their content instead, finding 2 becomes "declared heights are wrong" (Medium) and the band half of finding 6 goes away. The over- and under-height measurements hold either way.

## Verdict

The foundations are sound and on brief. Red is kept for action and selection, overdue uses brick, every specimen uses real Swiss-German content with tabular CHF amounts, radii are consistent (24 / 12 / 16), and the READMEs follow one structure that consumers can rely on. The cards are not yet safe to build from, though.

- The preview CSS has no `box-sizing` reset, so most bordered or padded controls render 2 to 12px larger than the HTML and README state.
- At their declared sizes, Button and TextField are cut off, losing the pending, disabled and TextArea specimens.
- Annotations use the same type as component text. Checkbox even writes state names into its labels.
- Status chips, selected chips and the field shadow are each drawn two or three different ways.
- Icon strokes vary from 1.2 to 1.75px.

Most of this can be fixed once, in `bundle.css` and a shared preview contract, rather than card by card. The Cover is the one card that is off the moodboard: it uses the brand red as a large decorative block, which the README forbids.

## READMEs

Every one of the 48 component READMEs has the same five sections: Covers, Spec, Our changes, Use and Don't. They name HeroUI parts and props, cite screen codes (C03, M08 …), and separate HeroUI defaults from local decisions. This is the strongest consumer asset in the set.

Problems to fix:

- **They contradict their cards in measurable places:** TextArea 88 vs 105.6, menu items 36 vs 48, Table row 45 vs 49, the Chip status label (lg + 14px icon) vs the Table chips (md + 12px), the Toast mobile action vs the inline-only card, and "≤ 4 primary tabs on a phone" vs four tabs that overflow.
- **Two READMEs claim the same screen with different specs:** ScrollShadow and TagGroup, for the C01/C02 rail.
- **Two sections are missing:** "Mobile (375)", which lists what changes on a phone, and "Build", a 3 to 6 line HeroUI JSX snippet with the props and token names to use. Agents would otherwise reconstruct both from inline styles.
- **Cover has no README.** Either add one that states its purpose and its colour exception, or remove the exception.

## Structure recommendation

1. **One preview contract, enforced in `bundle.css` and the harness.**
   - `body` carries the only ground.
   - Each card has three zones: a specimen zone with the component only; a state grid with fixed column widths, so lanes align and states never wrap into orphans; and an annotation strip in the reserved `.anno` style.
   - `bundle.css` gets the border-box reset and `.anno`.
   - The harness gets a check that fails a card when its content height differs from the declared height by more than 8px, or when its scrollWidth exceeds the declared width. Run it at the declared width and at 375.
2. **Shared snippets for the repeated elements:** field, button (each variant and size), chip / status label, menu item, close glyph and segmented group. Define each once (as `bundle.css` classes or copied verbatim from one source file) so that Fieldset, Drawer, Select and the others draw the same field as TextField.
3. **Regroup by the consumer's job.** Today Chip, Avatar and ColorPicker sit under "Date and data", so nobody looking for "status" finds Chip. Suggested groups:
   - **Foundations:** Typography, Icons (new), Surface
   - **Actions:** Button, ButtonGroup, ToggleButton, CloseButton, Link, Dropdown, Toolbar, Kbd
   - **Inputs:** TextField, InputGroup, NumberField, SearchField, InputOTP, Checkbox, RadioGroup, Switch, Slider, Fieldset, ColorPicker
   - **Pickers:** Select, ComboBox, Autocomplete, ListBox, DatePicker, Calendar, RangeCalendar
   - **Status and feedback:** Chip, Alert, Toast, Progress, EmptyState
   - **Overlays:** Modal, Drawer, AlertDialog, Popover, Tooltip
   - **Navigation:** Tabs, TagGroup, Breadcrumbs, Pagination, Accordion, Disclosure, ScrollShadow
   - **Content:** Card, Avatar, Table
4. **Add a Patterns group at 375px, built only from the cards above.** These are what consumers build first, and every moodboard favourite shows them:
   - tour tile and gift tile
   - stacked order row (C14) with its two status labels
   - category rail, once finding 7 is decided
   - section header with "Alle ansehen"
   - sticky price + CTA bar ("ab CHF 49.00 · Jetzt buchen", as in favourite #7 and the design-system review)
   - bottom sheet over a ghosted screen
5. **Add Mobile (375) and Build sections to each README**, and a README for the Cover.

## What is right

- **Colour discipline in the components.** Red appears only on primary actions, selected days, the active tab line and slider fill. Overdue and errors are brick (`#9A3412`), clearly separate from the brand red. Each status role has a soft background and a soft foreground, with contrast stated in `tokens.json`.
- **Content is real and local:** "CHF 54.00 bezahlen", "Platz reserviert · noch 12:40 Min.", "gemäss", "Grusskarte", DD.MM.YYYY dates, and tabular figures on amounts. The Drawer repeats the amount in its primary button, and AlertDialog states the consequence before the action. This is the money-confirm pattern done properly.
- **Lanes hold where they matter most.** In Table, header and body cells share exact x positions in all 5 columns (28/200/536/768/1060), and amounts are right-aligned with tabular figures. Alert icons and text share one lane across all 4 statuses. The Calendar grid uses fixed 36px columns. Checkbox, Radio and Switch descriptions indent exactly to the label (28px and 52px).
- **Radii follow the stated scale.** Buttons, cards and popovers use 24px, fields 12px, and items and chips 16px. Across all cards, 24px is the most used radius value (76 uses), then 12px (47) and 16px (38).
- **Serif and sans split as agreed.** Source Serif 4 appears only in titles ("Hidden Lucerne", Typography). Inter is used everywhere else. The Card tour tile is clean and close to the moodboard's white-card references.
- **Icon caps and joins are consistent**, with round caps on every open path. Only the stroke width needs unifying.
- **Open decisions are written down rather than hidden:** the focus colour, the soft Switch track and the unsourced 3D icon set.
