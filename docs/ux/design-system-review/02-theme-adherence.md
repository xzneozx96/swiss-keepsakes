# Design system review 02: theme and brand adherence

Reviewed 13.10.2026. Scope: `docs/ux/design-system/project/` (tokens.json, README.md, 48 component cards and Cover), compiled tokens in `docs/ux/design-system/review-harness/tokens.css`. File references below are relative to `docs/ux/design-system/project/` unless they name another folder; `:N` is a line number.

Sources of truth: `docs/ux/moodboard/type-and-colour.html` (approved theme B), `docs/ux/decisions.tsv` rows 23, 25, 26, 27, `docs/ux/ux-spec-phase1.md` §1, §2.3, §5.5, §10, `docs/ux/heroui-reference/README.md`, and the moodboard images (favourites #2, #4, #6, #7).

Method: every `var(--…)` and every literal colour in the 49 previews was listed by script and checked against the approved palette. All previews and READMEs were read. Contrast uses the WCAG 2.x formula. Colour distance is CIEDE2000 (ΔE00), where about 2 to 3 is the smallest difference most people notice side by side.

## Verdict

The palette values are correct. Every neutral, status colour and tint in `tokens.json` matches the approved theme B hex for hex. Overdue and errors use brick, never Swiss red. Serif is limited to titles, and German copy follows Swiss usage. The system still breaks the approved theme's central rule. Red and brick have similar lightness, and the approved draft keeps them apart only by form: red is a solid button, brick is a tint with an icon and words (`type-and-colour.html:134`). The system weakens that separation in four places: a red-tint selection style with off-palette #A3241A text that matches the brick overdue badge (ΔE00 2.8 for the tints, 5.6 for the texts), solid brick destructive buttons, a hover red that fails AA with white text (4.43:1), and a red focus ring with the same outline form as the brick invalid state. Red also appears as decoration in several cards, and the Chip specimen shows status words without icons. The theme is not yet faithful enough to build screens from. The fixes are small: token re-pointing plus colour and copy edits to about 20 previews, with no geometry changes.

## Findings

Severity: **High** breaks an approved rule or WCAG AA. **Medium** dilutes what red or brick means, or is an internal conflict in the system. **Low** covers copy and hygiene.

## Focus ring recommendation

**Recommendation: ink `#1A1A1A` (`color-focus` → `{color-foreground}`).** Keep the HeroUI geometry: a 2px ring with a 2px gap on buttons and controls, and 2px flush on fields.

| Option | Ring on #FFFFFF / #F7F7F5 | ΔE00 from brick #9A3412 | What it already means in this system |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keep red #D52B1E | 5.01 / 4.67 | 11.9 | Action and selection. The same hue family and the same outline form as the invalid state. |
| Ink #1A1A1A | 17.40 / 16.23 | 32.3 | Text. The approved selected chip (`type-and-colour.html:40`). No status meaning. |
| Info blue #1F5FA8 | 6.44 / 6.00 | 43.7 | The "Wartet" and "In Bearbeitung" status (Chip, Alert, Table). |

Contrast does not decide it: all three pass the 3:1 non-text minimum on every ground in use. These points do:

1. **Form.** The approved theme tells red from brick by form, not hue (`type-and-colour.html:134`). Focus and invalid share one form, a thin outline on the same field. TextField shows them side by side (`components/TextField/preview.html:6-7`): a 2px red ring next to a 1px brick border, ΔE00 11.9 apart. A user tabbing into a field sees a red-brown outline either way, and only the 12px text below says which state it is.
2. **Meaning.** Accent means action or selection (`README.md:14`). Focus is neither; it marks where the keyboard is. Blue already means "waiting or in progress", so a blue ring gives blue a second meaning and adds a second chromatic accent to every interactive state. That works against the single accent of the moodboard and theme B ("red replaces sage as the single accent", `type-and-colour.html:103`).
3. **Accent-filled controls.** On a primary button, a checked box, the selected day or a switch, a red ring is the control's own colour, so only the 2px gap marks focus. An ink ring stays visible around red, ink and grey controls.
4. **Scope.** Changing to ink is a colour-only change. It stays within "HeroUI geometry, our colours" (decisions.tsv row 25) and touches one token.

Implementation:

- `tokens.json:37`: `color-focus` = `{color-foreground}`. In every field preview, use `color-field-border-focus` (already defined at `tokens.json:29`) instead of `color-focus`.
- Invalid and focused stays a 2px brick outline. The FieldError gets its icon (finding 8), so the two states differ by hue, by the icon and by the words.
- DatePicker focused segment: replace the red tint with `color-default` and ink text (`components/DatePicker/preview.html:4`).
- Calendar: "today" is marked by the indicator dot, never by a ring, so a focused day and today look different (finding 1).
- Update `README.md:19`, `components/Button/README.md:18`, the TextField focus caption ("2px flush accent ring") and `heroui-reference/README.md:90`. Log the decision in decisions.tsv.
- Second choice, if the owner wants focus to look different from text: info blue. Do not keep red.

## What is right

- **Palette fidelity.** Every approved value is present and exact: background #F7F7F5, surface #FFFFFF, default #EFEFEC, line #DCDCD7, ink #1A1A1A, muted #5E5E5A, red #D52B1E, red tint #FCE9E7, success, warning, overdue and info with their tints, and hero sky #CFE4F5 to #FBF6E8. No preview uses an undefined token. The only literal brand hexes are on the ColorPicker card, where swatches are content.
- **Red never signals an error.** Alert, Toast, TextField, Checkbox, Table and Dropdown use brick for errors and overdue. The Alert, Toast and Link READMEs say outright: no red for errors, no red links. Links are ink with an underline.
- **The correct overdue badge exists.** Table renders "Überfällig seit 18.10." as a brick tint with a warning icon and words (`components/Table/preview.html:22`), and Alert and Toast pair the brick icon with plain words and a retry action.
- **Field borders follow the owner's call.** Text-like fields use #C4C4BF (hover #A6A6A1), and checkbox and radio edges use #8E8E89, exactly as decisions.tsv row 27 says.
- **Text contrast passes everywhere except the hover state.** Ink on background and surface is 16.2 to 17.4:1. Muted is 6.07:1 on background and 5.65:1 on default. Success, warning, overdue and info text on their tints is 5.5 to 6.4:1. White on red is 5.01:1 and white on brick is 7.31:1.
- **Type roles hold.** Serif appears only in Card, Typography and Cover. All UI text is Inter at HeroUI sizes, and field text is 16px on phones. The tokens match the approved scale: display 34/39, title 24/29, card-title 20/26 (all serif 500), heading 18/600, body 15/22, label 13/18 500, numbers 18/600. Every amount and date uses tabular figures.
- **Swiss German is right throughout.** No "ß" anywhere ("Grusskarte", "gemäss"). Money is "CHF 54.00". Dates are DD.MM.YYYY. The calendar starts on Monday with Mo–So. TWINT appears as a payment method. Buttons that move money name the amount ("CHF 37.80 erfassen"), and money sheets state the consequence before saving (spec §5.5).
- **The shape and feel match the moodboard.** The system has 24px pill buttons and cards, 12px fields, 16px chips, white cards with soft shadows and no borders, and calm warm-grey neutrals. ScrollShadow reproduces the approved chip rail (ink "Alle", white outlined chips). Card reproduces the approved tour tile: gradient photo slot, serif 20 title, 18/600 price, one red "Jetzt buchen". Every specimen has one primary action.
- **No emblems or borrowed styling.** There is no cross, no shield and no Victorinox crimson (#A00030), and the README carries the no-emblem rule.
