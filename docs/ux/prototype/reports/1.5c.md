# 1.5c: C04 cart line rebuilt as Lab L "Gift slip"

## 1. What I built

The owner chose lab option L, "Gift slip", for the C04 cart line, shown with frame "open". The coordinator settled the surface question (ask, answer option 1). The line card gets the agreed M02 surface. The order group has no card. Its title, policy, pickup and subtotal sit as open text on the page background.

- **Line (`GiftSlip`)**, `rounded-3xl bg-surface p-4 shadow-surface` with no border:
  - top row: 72px photo, name, and a bin button (`CartRemoveIcon`, danger, 44px);
  - under the name, in muted text: option and engraving ("Medium · Engraving “Anna”") and the unit price ("CHF 54.00 each");
  - a full-width row under a divider: a bordered − n + group (HeroUI `NumberField`, 44px buttons), with "Item total" and the line total on the right;
  - the invalid-date `Alert` and its `data-date-fix` button, inside the card under that row. The Alert is tinted `bg-warning-soft shadow-none`, because a white Alert on the white card lost its edges.
  - The card has no hover or focus ring because the card itself is not interactive. Its buttons have `focus-visible:focus-ring`.
- **Order group (`OrderGroup`)**, matching the lab's `CartSelectDemo` header: a serif title, then policy and "Requested pickup {date · place}" with 16px icons. Below that come the line cards (`gap-3`), then the subtotal row (`border-t`, from the lab's open-frame `OptionDemo`). Orders sit in a `gap-10` stack so that Order 1 and Order 2 read as separate groups.
- **Remove** still goes through the shared `useConfirm()` ConfirmDialog, with the same title and body as before.
- **Unchanged:** the empty state, the bottom bar, the blocked-continue message and focus handling.
- **Skeleton:** reshaped to a heading bar and two line-card blocks.

Not carried over from the lab, on purpose:
- the selection checkboxes and `CartSummarySheet` (round 3 selection, not asked for);
- the lab's hardcoded `language === "de"` strings. Every string now goes through `t()`.

Files changed:
- `front-end/src/features/cart/components/cart-view.tsx`: `OrderCard`/`LineItem` replaced by `OrderGroup`/`GiftSlip`. `CartLineView` gains `unit` and `totalLabel` and loses `removeLabel`.
- `front-end/src/features/cart/hooks/use-cart-view.ts`: adds the unit price and item-total label. The quantity label is now "Quantity, {name}".
- `front-end/src/routes/_guest/cart.tsx`: skeleton only.
- `front-end/src/i18n/en.ts`, `de.ts`: new keys `cart.line.quantityAria`, `cart.line.unit`, `cart.line.itemTotal`.
- New: `docs/ux/prototype/reports/1.5c-verify.cjs` (headless verification script).

## 4. Verification

I ran `node docs/ux/prototype/reports/1.5c-verify.cjs` (headless Chromium, 375px, EN and DE) against `vite --port 5199`. The cart was seeded with two lines: knife M with engraving "Anna" (full payment) and the gift box (30% deposit), both Wed 28 Oct, Shop Altstadt. Results, the same in EN and DE:

- Two orders render, "Order 1" and "Order 2" ("Bestellung 1" and "Bestellung 2"), with no horizontal overflow.
- Line text: "Kapellbrücke Taschenmesser | Mittel · Gravur «Anna» | CHF 54.00 pro Stück | Artikel gesamt | CHF 54.00". DE shows no English key strings.
- Pressing + on the gift box changes its item total from CHF 89.00 to CHF 178.00.
- The bin opens the ConfirmDialog: "Remove Kapellbrücke pocket knife · Medium?" / "… comes out of Order 1." Confirming removes the line, and the cart regroups to one order.
- Invalid date (knife set to Tue 13 Oct): the alert reads "Tue 13 Oct can no longer be requested", then "Needs 3 working days to engrave. The earliest pickup is Fri 16 Oct.", with the button "Request Fri 16 Oct instead". Pressing the button clears the alert.
- Empty state: "Your cart is empty." / "Ihr Warenkorb ist leer." with "Browse gifts". The skeleton was captured.
- Accessible names: "Remove Kapellbrücke pocket knife", "Decrease Quantity, …" on the − button, "Quantity, …" on the input and "Increase Quantity, …" on the + button. Tab order runs Back, Remove, −, input, +.
- No console errors.
