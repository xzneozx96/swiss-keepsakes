## 1. What was there and what changed

Codex's C14 (`tour-booking-list.tsx`, reached from the account hub's "Tour bookings" row) listed the guest's bookings with two chips each. C16 (`guest-tour-booking-page.tsx`, route `/account/bookings/$bookingId`) had the summary, money, actions, cancellation policy, payment history, timeline, a contact paragraph and the tour review. Gaps:

- No contact action: text only.
- No payment-policy snapshot.
- No reconciliation or session-closed view (2.7).
- An expired booking showed a balance.
- The fixed header covered the prototype strip.
- Several states could not be reached without the merchant steps.

Changes:

- **C16 sections:**
  - Payment policy snapshot: "30% deposit when booking, the rest by …" and the full and pay-later variants.
  - Cancellation policy with language fallback.
  - Contact the organiser, with Email and Call links (the shop's details, like gift C15) and the booking reference in the subject and body.
  - Expired and reconciliation notices.
  - Payment history rows with the paid time and, for late results, the received time and an "After expiry" chip.
  - Timeline labels for the new events.
  - In-flow sticky header, with the back label "Tour bookings".
- **Balance payment:** C16 → C12 shows no timer and no hold, and the amount is the balance. On success it returns to C16. While processing, the C16 action is disabled. The provider control answers it.
- **Sample bookings:** a prototype control, "Add sample tour bookings in every state", adds six signed-in-guest samples with `tb-demo-` ids:
  - held on 20 Oct, a real 15-minute hold;
  - expired, with a failed attempt;
  - expired with money after expiry;
  - delivered with a balance;
  - completed and paid;
  - no-show with payment kept.
  The past ones sit on the closed 13 Oct walk with one seat each, so future capacity is untouched. Reset removes them. Confirmed with an overdue balance is the seeded SK-T1042.
- **C14:** language-fallback tour names, and plural-aware group text (`useGroupText`, exported for reuse).

## 6. Demo assumptions and open questions

- Is the hub's two-row structure what the owner means by "alongside", or should C14 merge orders and bookings into one dated list? Merging would change the approved Phase 1 hub.
- The tour review on completed bookings is the reviews module's (not mine). That module still builds its group line from `tourBooking.group` ("1 children"). `useGroupText()` from `features/tours/booking/booking-summary.tsx` gives the plural-aware text.
- The organiser's contact uses the single demo shop. A real multi-merchant setup needs per-tour organiser details.
- The past samples fill the closed 13 Oct walk to exactly 12 of 12. If another seed booking is later added to that departure, "Add sample tour bookings" would push M05 over capacity for it. The samples are a review aid, not seed data.
- Mail rendering for N07, N09 and N22 on tours is worker C's. My drivers check that the right templates are sent, not how they render.
