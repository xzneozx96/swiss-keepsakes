- **The list (M03 shape).** Gift and tour reviews together, newest first. Both live in the same demo data list; tour reviews have a `tour:` id. The filter chips are All 89, Not replied 87 and Replied 2, then the line "Reviews can't be deleted. Reply to add the shop's side." Each card shows:
  - the gift or tour with its photo and "Gift" or "Tour"
  - the guest, stars and date
  - the review in the guest's language, or the other one with "Text in German" (AC30)
  - the shop's reply in a grey inset ("Reply from Ngọc · 14.10.2026"), with a language note when it is in the other language

  From 1024px the list is a table.
- **No delete.** There is no delete button or menu anywhere on the tab, at either width (AC17).
- **Reply and edit reply.** The sheet quotes the review and asks for:
  - **the reply's language:** English or German. It starts in the language the guest wrote in, or the interface language when the review exists in both.
  - **the reply:** an empty reply shows "Write a reply first."

  Edit reply opens with the current text and language, and "Save reply" replaces them. The reply stores its text, language, author (the signed-in staff member: Ngọc or Lukas) and date.
- **C03 and C09.** The existing review cards show the reply under the review text, indented like a C20 reply: "Reply from Ngọc", the date and the reply.
  - A reply in the other language adds "Text in German" / "Text auf Englisch", the same notice as the review text (AC30), and the reply text carries its own `lang`.
  - On the swipe cards the reply is clamped to three lines. "Show more" now also appears when the reply is long, and the reviews sheet shows it in full.
  - The guest's own review on C15 and C16 uses the same card, so it shows the reply too.
- **Empty states.** "No replies yet." and "Every review has a reply." With no reviews at all: "No reviews yet. Guests can review a gift or tour once it's completed."

## Decisions to confirm

- **Reply language.** The merchant picks it, and it is stored with the reply (the 4.1 model already has `reply.language`). The default is the guest's language. Guests reading in the other language see the AC30 notice; nothing is translated.
- **Editing a reply** replaces its text and language and records the editor and today's date, so the reply shows who last wrote it. There is no "remove reply". The spec asks for reply only, and a reply can be edited.
- **Card placement.** On the C03 and C09 swipe cards the reply sits under the review text, before the photos and the reviewer, so "Show more" covers both.
- **Filters.** The filters are All, Not replied and Replied, matching the spec's states. Gifts and tours share one list, each card naming its kind.
- **Long list, no paging.** The tab starts on All, so a phone shows all 89 review cards in one long page. Comments has the same at 43. Choose whether Not replied should be the default filter, or the list should page.
- **The Owner's public name** is "Ngọc" for new replies. The seeded reply on Marie's review says "Chị Ngọc" (catalog seed). See [4.12](4.12.md).
