# Step 1.9s report: M02 Today becomes Overview (Today + Analytics)

Owner request after 1.9r: "today page should become overview displaying both today and overview analytics … use recharts … make the dashboard richer and more visually appealing". The page is now **Overview**. The top part, **Today**, shows what needs action now. Below it, **Analytics** has a period switch, a KPI group, a composed chart of payments and orders, a status funnel, revenue by product group, payment methods and top pickup places. The route is still `#/store`.

## 0. Brief and plan (evon:ui-ux, "build directly" branch; dataviz skill)

**References (WebFetch of the four heroui.pro pages).** HeroUI Pro is a paid package and is not installed. I rebuilt its patterns with HeroUI v3, Tailwind and our tokens.

- **KPI:** title (`dt`), value (`dd`), trend chip and a sparkline below. The sparkline is an area chart with a 20%→0 gradient fill and a 2px stroke.
- **Widget:** a two-layer surface:
  - a `surface-secondary` shell with a header (title, description, legend or actions on the right) and an optional footer;
  - an elevated `bg-surface` content card with a subtle shadow.
- **KPI group:** several KPIs in one shared surface, with 1px separators between them.
- **Composed chart:** Recharts `ComposedChart` with Bar, Line and Area.
  - Grid lines are muted. Ticks are 10px muted with no axis or tick lines.
  - The tooltip uses a hover cursor line, and the active dot has a surface ring.

**Brief.** Same users and jobs as 1.9r: the merchant starts a shift on a phone or a laptop. The new job is "how is the shop doing over a period, compared with the period before" (the Shopify, Stripe and Square home pattern).

**Plan, built directly per the owner rule:**

1. **Overview header.** Title and today's date.
2. **Today section** (always first in the DOM, so Tab reaches the tiles first):
   - the overdue/owing alert card;
   - the four "To do" tiles;
   - a 3:2 row with Next pickups and Still to collect.
3. **Analytics section:**
   - a heading, a "range, compared with previous range" line, and period Tabs (7 days / 30 days / This month);
   - the KPI group of 7, with a hero KPI;
   - Payments and orders (the composed chart);
   - Order status (the funnel);
   - Revenue by product group, Payment methods and Top pickup places.

**Layout:**
- At 375px, everything stacks.
- From 1024px:
  - The KPI group is 4 across. The hero KPI spans 2 columns, so the 7 KPIs fill 2 full rows.
  - The analytics widgets form a 2-column grid. The chart spans the full width.
- From 1280px, the widgets go 3 across: the chart takes 2 of the 3 columns, with the funnel beside it.

**Removed from the Today part:** the 1.9r "Commission to pay" and "Completed this week" widgets. They now live in the KPI group as "Commission recorded" and the revenue and order KPIs, with a period and a delta. This avoids one concept showing two different numbers on the same page.

## 1. What I built

### Data

- **`lib/demo-history.ts` (new): DEMO HISTORY, clearly marked.**
  - 163 closed orders: 80% completed, 11% declined, 9% cancelled.
  - They cover the 63 days before the demo start (11.08–12.10). Sundays are quiet, and volume grows gently.
  - The history is deterministic: a fixed-seed PRNG anchored to `DEMO_START`, built once at module scope.
  - Every history order is a valid T0 `Order`, so `orderTotal`, `amountPaid`, `commission` and `balance` apply to it. Refs look like `SK-H001`, and nothing links to them.
  - **The history never enters `data.orders`.** It is merged only inside the analytics hook. M02 tasks, M03 tabs, P02 numbers and stock therefore cannot change; the counts in §4 confirm this.
  - The history is merged only while `data.orders` is non-empty. A cleared demo shows the analytics empty state.
- **`lib/analytics.ts` (new):** pure functions over T0 selectors.
  - `periodRanges`:
    - 7d and 30d end today; the previous period is the same length right before it.
    - "This month" runs from the 1st to today. Its previous period is the same days of the month before (1–13 Sept).
  - `overviewAnalytics` computes everything below per Zurich calendar day. Definitions:
    - **Revenue requested:** `orderTotal` of orders created that day, all statuses.
    - **Revenue accepted:** `orderTotal` by `acceptedAt` day, excluding orders cancelled since.
    - **Collected:** succeeded payments by payment day.
    - **Still to collect:** the level at the end of each day: accepted and not closed by then, minus payments made by then. For today this equals T0 `balance` (CHF 174.30, the same as 1.9r and the Today widget). The KPI is the level on the last day; its delta compares with the level at the end of the previous period.
    - **Commission recorded:** T0 `commission().total` where the state is `recorded`, by `commissionRecordedAt` day.
    - **Orders:** count created that day.
    - **Average order:** requested revenue ÷ orders.
    - **Funnel:** the cohort of orders requested in the period. Steps are requested → accepted (has `acceptedAt`) → paid (`amountPaid > 0`) → completed, plus declined and cancelled.
    - **Product groups:** line totals by product `categoryId`, for the period's orders excluding declined and cancelled.
    - **Payment methods:** succeeded payments in the period, by method.
    - **Pickup places:** `orderTotal` and count by `pickupPlaceId`, for the period's orders excluding declined and cancelled.
- **`hooks/use-overview-analytics.ts` (new):** the period state and the view model.
  - Money goes through `formatMoney`.
  - Ranges use `Intl.DateTimeFormat.formatRange`: "7–13 Oct", "14 Sept – 13 Oct"; DE "7.–13. Okt.".
  - Deltas use a percent format with a sign: "+12%", "−5%".
  - When the previous period is 0, the chip shows "–", with the sr text "Nothing to compare with in {range}". It never shows ∞.
  - Delta colour follows direction × polarity:
    - up is good for revenue, collected, orders and average;
    - down is good for still to collect;
    - commission is neutral.

### UI

- **`components/overview-analytics.tsx` (new):**
  - `Widget`: the HeroUI Pro widget anatomy.
  - `KpiGroup` and `Kpi`: one surface with `gap-px` dividers. Each KPI has a label, the value, a delta chip with a trend icon and an sr-only sentence, and a decorative sparkline (`aria-hidden`). Money KPIs are blue; the count KPI is ink.
  - `TrendWidget`: the composed chart, described below.
  - `FunnelWidget`, `ShareWidget` (product groups, pickup places) and `MethodsWidget`, described below.
  - `PeriodTabs`: HeroUI `Tabs`, the same pattern as P02.
  - The analytics empty state.
- **The composed chart (`TrendWidget`):** two panels share the day axis and one synced tooltip.
  - The top panel is Collected CHF: an area with a gradient wash and a 2px line.
  - The bottom panel is Orders: bars with a 4px top radius, today drawn at 35% because the day is still running.
  - Grids are horizontal and solid 1px `--border`. Ticks are 11px muted.
  - The tooltip card shows the date heading, then value and series for both measures.
  - Focus and arrow keys use the Recharts accessibility layer. The svg has a `title` and a `desc` (the period summary).
  - "Show table" switches to a day table (day, collected, orders), and an sr-only summary line is always present.
- **`FunnelWidget`:** ordinal ink ramp (100/70/50/35%) for requested → completed. "Not going ahead" (declined, cancelled) is muted. Each row shows count · share. Both lists are drawn on one scale, where requested = 100%.
- **`ShareWidget` and `MethodsWidget`:**
  - Product groups and pickup places are Recharts horizontal bar lists: the name above the bar, the value on the right, the share in the tooltip, and an sr-only table.
  - Payment methods is one stacked bar with 2px surface gaps between parts, plus a legend list (dot, method, count of payments, CHF, share).
- **`components/merchant-today.tsx`:**
  - The Today part is wrapped in a `section` with the heading "Today". The "To do" heading is now sr-only.
  - Next pickups and Still to collect use the new `Widget` shell, on a 3:2 grid from lg.
  - Removed the `CommissionWidget`, `CompletedWidget` and `WidgetHeader` components and the `WeekBar` type.
  - New props: `todayHeading` and `analytics`.
- **`hooks/use-merchant-today.ts`:** dropped the commission and completed view model. The `partners` import became unused and is removed.
- **`routes/store/index.tsx`:** now `OverviewPage`; passes `analytics={<OverviewAnalytics …/>}` and `todayHeading`.
