# C19 Stories

14 October 2026. Step 4.3 is built and awaiting owner review. Built directly in the React app.

## Review the changes

Open `#/stories` at phone width, or "See all" on the C01 stories rail, then switch EN and DE.

- **Layout matches C02.** A 260px photo banner with the serif title "Local stories" / "Geschichten aus Luzern", a short intro and the count ("4 stories" / "4 Geschichten"). Below it, the rounded sheet lists one story per row: a 4:3 photo, serif title, a two-line excerpt and the read time. C08 already repeats this structure the same way.
- **Current language only (R02).** EN lists the four published English stories, including the EN-only Water Tower. DE lists the four German ones, including the DE-only "Luzern bei Nacht". Drafts never show. Newest first.
- **Empty state.** With no story published in the current language: "There are no stories in English yet." / "Es gibt noch keine Geschichten auf Deutsch.", with one action, "Back to Explore". The banner then hides the count, and C01 hides its stories rail.
- **Loading.** Three skeleton cards, as on C02.
- **One story card.** The new `StoryCard` has a `rail` layout (C01, unchanged look) and a `list` layout (C19). It replaces C01's `TeaserCard`, which is deleted.

Cards still open `/stories` until C20 exists. Step 4.4 points them, and the C01 story rows, at `/stories/{slug}`.
