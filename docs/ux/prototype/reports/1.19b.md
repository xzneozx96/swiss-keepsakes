### How the run works

One headless Chromium session against `npx vite --port 5202 --strictPort` with HMR off.

1. Prototype bar → **Reset demo**, then "Via Hotel am See QR".
2. As the guest: knife (Medium, engraving "Anna", 21 Oct, Altstadt) and gift box (20 Oct, Altstadt) through C03, C04 **Select all**, C05 email code, C06 Send, C07 skip. This creates SK-1043 (knife) and SK-1044 (gift box).
3. Role switch to Merchant · Store Manager, then everything below through the real screens.

No localStorage writes and no clock pinning; the only reads of `sk.demo` are to find order refs and statuses. Times in the shots are therefore the live demo clock (10:00–10:03), not the 10:30/10:35 of the outline.
