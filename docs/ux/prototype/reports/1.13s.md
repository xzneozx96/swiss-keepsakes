**Computed-style check.** `1.13s-drive.mjs` first reads an M02 card on `#/store` as the reference:
- border 0px
- radius 24px
- box-shadow `… rgba(0,0,0,0.04) 0px 2px 4px 0px, rgba(0,0,0,0.06) 0px 1px 2px 0px, rgba(0,0,0,0.06) 0px 0px 1px 0px`

It then finds every boxed element on C15: a surface background with a radius of 12px or more, or a solid border on all four sides. Alerts, the tab list, buttons and links are excluded, and so are small shapes under 48px wide (the stepper dots). For each box it checks that the border width is 0, the box-shadow equals M02's, and the radius equals M02's. It also checks that nothing overflows horizontally and that DE shows no raw i18n keys.
