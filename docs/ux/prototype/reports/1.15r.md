# Step 1.15r report: M04 next step + M08 Record payment, redesign

Owner verdict on 1.15: "redesign the ui entirely … should be the same style with the page from M04". This step moves the fulfilment actions and the M08 sheet into the 1.11r style. All T0 transitions, guards and button texts stay as they were.

## 0. How I used the evon:ui-ux skill

- **Skill choice:** `Skill evon:ui-ux` is the right skill for M04 and M08, because they are back-office screens. The owner's 1.15 comment says "impeccable", but the coordinator confirmed that the owner's rule applies: dashboard screens use evon.
- **Question 1 (route):** "redesign" goes to branch `U`. I sent gate 1 (brief and plan) to the coordinator with `orca orchestration ask`.
  - Answer: "Just build it. One constraint: open decision D1 defaults progress bars to ink, not red. The owner skipped all evon wireframe gates."
  - So I built without wireframes and made the step bar ink.
- **Question 2 (audit):** this reuses what 1.11r set up:
  - `ActionPanel` and `DetailCard` (`order-detail/components/detail-card.tsx`)
  - `SheetFrame` and the 1.11r choice-card class
  - `StatusChip`, `ProposalMark`, HeroUI NumberField, TimeField and RadioGroup
  - No new libraries, no new icons, no token changes.
- **Question 3 (style):** the project style, the skill's direction B (soft cards, no borders). The only dashed frame is the one around the locked Complete, which says "this is fenced off".
- **Reference read:** `references/layouts/app.md` ("Trang chi tiết bản ghi"). Rules applied:
  - One primary action per state.
  - An action tied to a value sits next to that value: Record payment sits under the "Still owed" panel.
- **Self-check, the skill's five questions** (on the 375 and 1024 shots):
  1. The step bar and money panel are the same shape in every status.
  2. All actions are buttons.
  3. Nesting: soft panels inside the action card only, one level.
  4. The 1024 columns are balanced, as in 1.11r.
  5. The heaviest element is the one primary button of the current step.
- **Two fixes after looking at the shots:**
  - At 1024, the German step names under each bar truncated ("Angeno…", "In Vorber…"). The bar now has only the "Step 3 of 5 · Ready" line under it, with step names for screen readers.
  - The M08 summary card at first took 170px. The amount field takes focus when the sheet opens, which scrolled the card out of view. It is now a single 76px row.

### Brief

- **Users:** the Owner and the Manager, at the counter.
- **Main job once an order is accepted:** see the step the order is on and what is still owed, then do the next step (Ready, Handed over, Complete, Record payment) in one tap.
- **Main job in M08:** record cash or a transfer correctly, and see the consequence before saving.

## 1. What I built

### M04 next step (`fulfilment-actions.tsx`)

The block now uses the `ActionPanel` from 1.11r, so it has the same place and shape as the decision:
- the band in the progress tone
- "Your next step"
- a title for each status

| Status | Title (EN / DE) | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| accepted | Wait for the first payment / Auf die erste Zahlung warten | Record payment (primary) |
| preparing | Prepare the gifts / Geschenke vorbereiten | Mark as ready, Record payment |
| ready, owed | Hand over at {place} / Übergabe: {place} | Mark as handed over (ProposalMark), Record payment, Complete locked "Not handed over yet" |
| ready, paid | Hand over at {place} | Handed over and complete, with its consequence line |
| handed_over, owed | Collect the balance / Restbetrag einziehen | Record payment (primary), Complete locked "CHF 62.30 still owed" |
| handed_over, paid | Close the order / Bestellung schliessen | Complete order (primary), with its consequence line |
| completed | Order completed | "Completed by Lukas · …" |

Every status (except completed) shows the same three parts:
- **Step bar:** 5 segments in ink (D1), `aria-current="step"`, and "Step 4 of 5 · Handed over" underneath.
- **Money panel:** "Still owed CHF 62.30 · Paid CHF 26.70 of CHF 89.00", or "Paid in full CHF 89.00".
- **Hint and buttons.**

Other details:
- **Locked Complete:** a dashed frame holds the disabled button (tertiary, ink) and "Complete is locked:" with the T0 reason chip (`aria-describedby`).
- **Focus hand-off after each step:** unchanged. Focus goes to the next enabled button, or to the panel heading if there is none.

### M08 Record payment sheet (`record-payment-sheet.tsx`, `use-record-payment.ts`)

- **Summary row on top:** "Still owed" in `text-title`, with Total and Paid on the right. "Still owed: CHF 62.30" stays as one sentence for screen readers.
- **Amount field:** NumberField in CHF, defaulting to the balance.
- **Duplicate warning:** a warning Alert, shown before the tap.
- **Method:** choice cards with an ink frame when selected, the same as the M04 change and decline sheets.
- **Received at:** a 24h TimeField, capped at the demo time.
- **Note:** optional.
- **Consequence button:** unchanged, including "… again …" for a duplicate. The button is disabled when the amount is above the balance.
- **Hook:** adds `total` and `paid` (T0 `orderTotal` and `amountPaid`). It has no money logic of its own.
