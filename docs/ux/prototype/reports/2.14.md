## 1. What was there and what changed

Nothing existed for overdue balances or tour emails. Tour mail would have rendered as raw parameters in the Mail panel.

**Overdue policy.** There is a new optional policy, snapshotted per departure and per booking:

```ts
TourOverduePolicy = { action: "cancel" } | { action: "grace"; graceHours }
```

With no policy, the merchant decides. `balanceOverdue()` (shared selector) gives each confirmed booking that owes money past its deadline one stage:

| Stage | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cancel` | Cancel policy, before the tour starts | Cancelled by the check |
| `grace` | Grace period still running | Nothing yet |
| `merchant` | No policy, grace over, or the tour already started | The merchant decides |

A cancel policy never cancels a tour that has already happened.

**Overdue check.** `cancelOverdueBookings()` cancels only `cancel`-stage bookings. It releases their seats by status, logs `cancelled-overdue` by system, and sends N11 to the guest and to staff (staff mail in the staff UI language). It is idempotent. Merchant screens (M02, M05, M06, M07) run it while open, through `useOverdueCheck`, standing in for a scheduler. No saved card is ever charged.

**Reminder job.** `sendTourReminders()` sends:

- N10 at the R07 proposed steps, 3 days and 1 day before the deadline, for confirmed bookings that still owe money.
- N13 the day before the tour.

Each email is sent once per booking and step. The job runs from a prototype control, because the timing is a proposal. M07 labels the reminder timing "proposal, not yet approved", inside a `ProposalMark`.

**Fixtures** (new, relative to the demo start of 13 Oct, 10:00):

- `walk-14`: cancel policy, deadline 13 Oct 18:00, tour 14 Oct 10:00.
  - SK-T2001 (Anna): deposit paid, CHF 156.10 owed.
  - SK-T2002 (Yuki): paid in full.
- `lake-15`: pay later with 48 hours' grace, deadline 12 Oct 18:00.
  - SK-T2003 (Daniel, DE): in grace until 14 Oct 18:00.

**Prototype control.** "Tours · merchant operations" adds two buttons:

- "Skip past this booking's balance deadline" moves the demo clock to one minute after the deadline of the booking on screen. This is the same mechanism as B's +16 min skip. The check then runs at once and the booking's snapshot is left unchanged.
- "Run the reminder job (N10, N13)".

**Emails.** The mail helpers `mailTourGuest` and `mailTourStaff` (`merchant/lib/mail.ts`) add tour emails to the existing gift mail log (`sendMail`) with a new `bookingId`. `renderMail` hands any mail with a `bookingId` to `renderTourMail` (`merchant/lib/render-mail.ts`), which renders EN/DE copy in the recipient's language. German uses Sie. Worker B calls the same helpers for N07, N08, N09 and N22 inside `pay` and `createBooking`, as the coordinator decided.

## 6. Demo assumptions and open questions

- **Press Reset demo** (prototype bar) to see the new fixtures. `DEMO_VERSION` was not bumped, so a browser that already holds demo data keeps its old tour state until Reset.
- **Known inconsistency.** The shared payment chip (`components/booking-status.tsx`, B's logic) still says "Deposit paid · balance due" on a booking cancelled for overdue, while M07 says nothing more is owed. I reported it to the coordinator for B and did not edit it.
- **Guest screens.** They do not run the overdue check. A guest who opens C16 after the deadline, before any merchant screen runs the check, sees "Balance overdue" rather than "Cancelled". A real scheduler removes this.
- **Global skip.** "Skip past this booking's balance deadline" moves time for everything (gift deadlines, holds) by about 8 hours in the walk-14 case. Reset restores the clock.
- **For the owner:**

  - the grace length
  - whether a cancel policy should also apply once the tour has started
  - what happens to money already paid on an overdue cancellation (refund rules, Phase 3)
