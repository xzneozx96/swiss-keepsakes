## 1. What changed visually (`features/tours/merchant/manifest-page.tsx`)

**Header, now in the M04 `OrderHeader` look.**

- The back link "All departures" is a muted text link with the arrow icon. Before, it was an underlined link.
- `h1` "Guest list", then the tour name, date and seat/collect line as sub-lines.
- The phase chip sits right-aligned from 1024px, where M04 puts its chips.

**Notices.**

- The HeroUI Alerts stay as they are (results to record, cancelled departure).
- The "results can be recorded after …" line gets M04's info-note style: an info icon and muted text.

**Booking cards (phones), in the M03 ticket look.**

- **Surface.** The M03/M02 card surface with no border.
- **Header.** The guest name as the ticket title. The booking reference (link to M07) and the phone (`tel:`) on the line under it.
- **Body.** The booking and payment chips, then the group line.
- **Footer.** A dashed rule, then the money as one fact per row:
  - Booking total (bold, like M03's Order total)
  - Received
  - Outstanding balance
- **Actions.** The result buttons and the locked Complete (unchanged M04 1.15r pattern) sit under the money.
- **Not a link.** The card holds buttons and links, so it cannot be one link like the M03 ticket.

**Money (cards and the 1024 table).**

- "Paid X of Y" and "Outstanding balance: Z" became three label/value rows. They use the existing keys `tourMerchant.total`, `tourMerchant.paid` and `tourMerchant.balance`.
- The rows wrap: in the narrow table column the amount drops under its label, so the Outcome column stays fully visible at 1024 in DE.
- `tourMerchant.paidOf` became unused and was removed (EN, DE).

**Overdue balance (2.14).** It now shows as the M04 decision-block danger pill (soft brick, warning icon) instead of a red text line.

**From 1024px.** The Table stays (M03 keeps its Table). Its secondary reference text now uses `text-label`, as M03 does.
