**Booking cards (phones), in the M03 ticket look.**

- **Surface.** The M03/M02 card surface with no border.

  - Booking total (bold, like M03's Order total)
  - Received
  - Outstanding balance

- **Not a link.** The card holds buttons and links, so it cannot be one link like the M03 ticket.

**Money (cards and the 1024 table).**

- "Paid X of Y" and "Outstanding balance: Z" became three label/value rows. They use the existing keys `tourMerchant.total`, `tourMerchant.paid` and `tourMerchant.balance`.
- The rows wrap: in the narrow table column the amount drops under its label, so the Outcome column stays fully visible at 1024 in DE.
- `tourMerchant.paidOf` became unused and was removed (EN, DE).

**Overdue balance (2.14).** It now shows as the M04 decision-block danger pill (soft brick, warning icon) instead of a red text line.

**From 1024px.** The Table stays (M03 keeps its Table). Its secondary reference text now uses `text-label`, as M03 does.

## Round 3 follow-up (14 October 2026)

Coordinator follow-up: fix the M06 1024 DE overflow on walk-13, with no change at 375 and none in EN beyond the fix. Backup: [2.11-r3-followup-original/](2.11-r3-followup-original/).

- **Cause.** HeroUI buttons are `whitespace-nowrap`. The locked "Buchung abschliessen" box needs at least 209px, which pushed the DE table to 712px in a 696px frame.
- **Change (`attendance-actions.tsx`, the locked Complete button in the M06 list only).** From 1024 the button may wrap, and only when the page is German: `lg:[&:lang(de)]:whitespace-normal`. `<html lang>` follows the language switch.
  - `lg:h-auto lg:min-h-9 lg:py-2 lg:text-center` keep a one-line button at exactly 36px and centre a wrapped label.
  - M07 (`detailed`) and every other button are untouched.
- **Why German only, and why only that button.** I tried two broader versions first, and the follow-up compare rejected both:
  - **Wrapping in both languages.** Any wrapping lowers the column's minimum width, and the table's auto layout then re-balances every column. In EN, lake-11's "Complete booking" broke onto two lines.
  - **Wrapping all M06 buttons in DE.** On departures that never overflowed, such as walk-18, "Leistung erbracht" wrapped while "Nicht erschienen" did not, so the two buttons had different heights.
- **Table width, every departure, EN and DE** ([2.11-r3-table-width.mjs](2.11-r3-table-width.mjs), log [2.11-r3-table-width.out](2.11-r3-table-width.out)): every guest-list table now measures 696/696, including walk-13 in DE.
