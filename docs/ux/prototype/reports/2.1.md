## 6. Demo assumptions and open questions

- **Sample values only.** Age bands, prices, capacities, deadlines and participant questions are samples, not merchant settings.
- **Infant seats are one rule for every tour.** Adults and children take a seat, infants do not (`participantSeats`). Making this per tour would change seat counting for every screen. Owner question: should infants ever take a seat?
- **Payment policy is per departure.** The walk has deposit and full-payment departures. PRD 9.1 says policy is configured per product. Owner question: per tour or per departure? C09 now explains the mix (see `2.3.md`).
- **The 30% deposit is fixed** in `firstPaymentAmount`.
- **Older browsers need Reset.** A browser holding demo data from before this change shows the new tours, departures and seat fillers only after Reset demo. It never crashes.
- **Worker C's `lake-15` is bookable with a past balance deadline.** It is a pay-later departure with balance due 12 Oct, before the demo start, so C10 shows "Due by Mon 12 Oct" and a new booking would be overdue at once. I reported this to the coordinator; it is worker C's file.
- **The seeded fillers are data only.** Their contact details and money appear on M05/M06 as sample bookings.
