# Step 1.8 report: C07 Request sent (+ optional trip profile)

## 1. What I built

### C07 Request sent (`#/cart/sent?orders=<ids>`)

C06 opens C07 with `replace`, using the contract from 1.7. C07 hides the tab bar and shows a skeleton while it loads.

- **Header.** A success icon, "Request sent", and "Your request went to the seller as 2 separate orders. Nothing has been charged." (there is a singular form for one order).
- **Your orders.** One row per created order. Only the signed-in guest's own orders are shown; unknown ids are dropped. Each row links to that order's C15 (`/account/orders/$orderId`) and shows:
  - photo, first gift (+N more), order total and reference
  - "Requested pickup: Wed 21 Oct · Shop Altstadt"
  - T0 progress chip "Awaiting seller confirmation" and payment chip "Nothing to pay yet"
  - the payment deadline rule, using C06's exact wording (`checkout.rule.*`, read-only), with the automatic cancel and, for deposits, the balance and its date
- **"See all in My transactions"** links to `/account` (C14).
- **What happens next**, in three numbered steps:
  1. The seller confirms each order. Dates and places stay requests until then. We email you when the seller answers. No response time is given (R03).
  2. You then pay what is due per order; an unpaid order is cancelled automatically.
  3. You pick up on the confirmed date and place.
- **Trip profile** (below).
- **Fixed bottom bar:** "Back to Explore" opens `/`.
- **Empty state.** When `?orders=` is missing, unknown or belongs to another guest, C07 shows the shared EmptyState with "There's no new request to show here." and "Back to Explore".

### Trip profile (spec §4.9, R09, AC20)

A card titled "Help us understand your trip", with the note "Optional: 3 short questions. Your answers don't change your pickup dates."
- **Open:**
  - Radio: "With a tour group" / "On my own"
  - "When are you in Lucerne?" with two rows, Arrival and Departure. Each row opens a bottom sheet with one Calendar.
  - Buttons: "Save answers" (disabled until something is answered) and "Skip"
  - Every question is optional on its own. Past days are allowed, because guests are often already in Lucerne. Departure cannot be before arrival, and arrival cannot be after departure.
- **Answered:** "Thanks, your answers are saved." Below that, a summary (Travelling / Arrival / Departure, with "Not answered" for gaps) and "Edit answers". Editing reuses the form, with "Back" instead of "Skip".
- **Skipped:** "You skipped the trip questions." and "Answer now". This survives reload.

### Data

- New `features/checkout/types.ts` defines `TripProfile { travelStyle?, arriveOn?, leaveOn?, skippedAt? }`.
- `DemoData` gets an **optional** field, `tripProfiles?: Record<guestId, TripProfile>`. No `DEMO_VERSION` bump: stored state without the field reads as "not answered". Reset clears it because the seed has no such field.
- A skip only counts for orders created before it, so the guest's next request asks again. Answers are kept per guest, so a later C07 shows them.
