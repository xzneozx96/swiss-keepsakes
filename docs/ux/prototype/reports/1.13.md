# Step 1.13: C15 Guest order detail

## 1. What I built

**Screen:** C15 Guest order detail at `#/account/orders/<orderId>` (for example `#/account/orders/sk-1044`). It is the route C14 links to. The tab bar is hidden, as on other detail screens. The screen shows, top to bottom:

- product photo and name, with the progress label and the payment label (T0 `useOrderSummary`)
- one notice per state (HeroUI Alert)
- the action block
- requested or confirmed pickup: date, place, address, hours, instructions
- the gifts as ordered (AC15 snapshot) with total, paid and still to pay
- payment history
- the progress timeline: logged steps with times, then the upcoming milestones
- the policy at the time of ordering (`order.paymentPolicy` and `order.cancellationPolicy`, never the product's current policy)
- "Questions about this order?": the order reference as selectable text with a Copy button, plus Email (mailto with the reference in the subject) and Call

**Action block.** Each action is gated on a T0 guard:
- **Pay CHF X** appears only when `startOnlinePaymentBlock` returns null. It is hidden while a payment is processing and on closed orders.
- **Cancel order** appears only when `cancelBlock(order, "guest")` returns null, that is, on an accepted order with nothing paid and nothing processing. It asks first in the shared `ConfirmDialog` (`useConfirm`).
- **Request again** appears on cancelled orders. It puts each line back in the cart with its original date and place, then opens C04. A date that is now too early shows C04's invalid-date fix. Nothing is sent automatically.
- **Write a review** appears on completed orders. It is a disabled slot labelled "Coming in a later step" until step 1.16 builds C18.

**Pay link for step 1.14:** `#/account/orders/<id>/pay`. It is a plain href because the route does not exist yet. **Step 1.14:** name the file `routes/_guest/account_.orders.$orderId_.pay.tsx`. The trailing `_` after `$orderId` keeps it from nesting under C15, which has no `<Outlet/>`. Then switch `actions.pay.href` in `use-order-detail.ts` to a typed Link.

**States and how to reach each one.** Sign in as Anna, then use Prototype controls → Order detail → **Add sample orders in every state**. This adds SK-1043 to SK-1050; the refs assume a fresh Reset.

The emails these transitions send are dropped, so the Mail panel shows only the presenter's own actions. The samples hold stock like real orders: knife M ×1, knife S ×1, gift box ×1 (another gift box is handed over), and candle box ×1, so C03 availability drops while they exist. Reset removes them.

## 5. Open questions for the owner

1. **Duplicated payment line on accepted orders.** The payment chip ("Payment due · CHF 54.00 by …") repeats in the warning notice ("Pay CHF 54.00 by …"). The chip keeps the two-label rule, and the notice carries the auto-cancel consequence. Should the chip stay?
2. **The free-text reasons and the print stay in English in DE.** The decline reason and the print's name and policy appear in English in DE. The policy shows the "Text auf Englisch" notice, but the name and reason have no notice.
3. **Sample orders change stock.** The sample orders hold real stock, so C03 shows fewer knives and gift boxes until Reset. Is that acceptable for a presenter control, or should samples be restricted to products the demo path doesn't use?
4. **Pay on pay-later orders.** "Pay CHF 24.00" also appears on ready pay-later and handed-over orders (T0 allows online payment of the balance, spec §4.4). Keep it, or show it only for first payments in flow 1?
