# C01 Discovery home, finished

14 October 2026. Step 4.2 is built and awaiting owner review. Built directly in the React app; no mockups.

## Review the changes

Open `#/` at phone width, then switch EN and DE.

- **Time blocks.** The first two rails are now titled "Have 2 hours in Lucerne?" / "Zwei Stunden in Luzern?" and "A few days here?" / "Ein paar Tage hier?" (spec §4.1). The rails are unchanged. Each block ends with one story row: the Musegg walk under the 2-hour block and the steamboat story under the few-days block. A row is a thumbnail, a serif title and "Story · 4 min read". It is a row, not a card, so the rail stays the block's main content.
- **Stories rail.** It lists the published articles in the current language, newest first, from the 4.1 demo data. EN shows four (with the EN-only Water Tower story); DE shows four (with the DE-only "Luzern bei Nacht"). The "Coming soon" badge is gone. If a language has no published story, the rail is hidden.
- **Floating Trip advice button.** An ink pill, "Trip advice" / "Reisetipps", bottom-right above the tab bar on C01 and above the booking bar on C09. It opens `/plan` (C21, step 4.5). Ink rather than Swiss red, so each screen keeps one red action: the advice banner on C01, "Choose a departure" on C09. C20 gets the same button in step 4.4 (D1).
- **Published gifts only.** C01 now skips gifts marked unpublished (4.1's `isPublished`), ready for M12.
- **Search.** Global search finds stories in the real articles of the current language, by title or excerpt, without "Coming soon".

Story cards and rows open `/stories` until C19 (step 4.3) and C20 (step 4.4) exist. Step 4.4 points them at `/stories/{slug}`.
