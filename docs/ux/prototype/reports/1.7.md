# Step 1.7 report: C06 Review and send request

## 1. What I built

### C06 Review and send (`#/cart/review`)

C06 hides the tab bar, shows a skeleton on load, and has a sticky header with a back button to the cart.

**Top of screen.** An intro note: "Nothing is charged now. The seller confirms each order first, then you pay what is due for that order." If any gift's text falls back to the other language, a language notice follows (R02, AC30).

**One card per order** shows:
- the title ("Order 1"), using the same numbering as C04
- the policy line, using C04's wording
- the requested pickup date and place
- items: photo, name, option, engraving, "1 × CHF 54.00" and the line total
- **Order total**
- **Due after the seller confirms** (CHF 54.00 / CHF 26.70 / CHF 0.00 for pay later)
- the **payment deadline rule**, which includes the automatic cancel. For a deposit it also gives the balance and its date ("The rest (CHF 62.30) is due by Sat 24 Oct"). For pay later it gives the amount and its date.
- the **cancellation policy**, as it is snapshotted on the order

**Bottom bar.** "Combined total, for reference" CHF 143.00, and a full-width **"Send request · no payment yet"** button.

**States:**
- **Stock changed on send.** Nothing is sent and the cart is unchanged. A danger Alert (`role="alert"`, which takes focus and scrolls into view) names each short item, for example "Kapellbrücke pocket knife · Medium: sold out", or "…: only 1 left, you asked for 2". It has an "Edit cart" button that opens C04.
- **Invalid date on send.** If the demo clock passed a date between C04 and C06, a similar Alert asks the guest to choose a new date in the cart.
- **Empty cart.** This covers direct visits and Back after sending. The screen redirects to `/cart`, which shows its empty state.
- **Signed out on a direct visit.** Send opens the sign-in sheet. After signing in, the guest taps Send again; nothing is sent automatically.

### Cart Continue (C04 → C05 → C06)

Continue calls `useRequireSignIn().requireSignIn(() => navigate("/cart/review"))`:
- Signed out: the sign-in sheet opens, and after sign-in the guest lands on C06 with the cart unchanged (verified by comparing the cart before and after).
- Signed in: C06 opens straight away.

### `features/checkout/lib/create-orders.ts` (pure, `DemoState → DemoState`)

- **`buildOrderDrafts(state, {now, language})`** turns the cart into complete `Order` objects. It uses `groupCartIntoOrders` from step 1.5 and snapshots, per AC15:
  - per line: product name, option label, unit price, quantity, engraving, commission rate
  - per order: payment policy and cancellation policy, plus the partner from `attributedPartnerId` (last click within 30 days)
  - also: `guestId`, `language` = the UI language, `status: "requested"`, and `log: [requested · guest · now]`
  - refs and ids: refs are `SK-${nextOrderNumber + i}` and ids are the lowercased ref, the same convention as the 1.13 samples. Line ids are `${orderId}-n`, so the function is deterministic and safe when StrictMode runs it twice.
  - **C06 renders these same drafts**, and all amounts come from T0 selectors (`orderTotal`, `lineTotal`, `firstPaymentAmount`, `dueDatesAtAcceptance`). The screen cannot differ from what gets stored.
- **`sendBlock(state, now)`** returns `empty`, `signed-out`, `invalid-date` or `stock-changed`. The stock check adds up quantities per option across the whole cart and compares them with `variantAvailable` (on hand minus units held by accepted orders). **Nothing is reserved (AC05).**
- **`createOrdersFromCart(state, ctx)`**:
  - re-checks `sendBlock` on the latest state and returns the state unchanged if it is blocked
  - otherwise appends the orders, increases `nextOrderNumber` and empties the cart
  - for each order, sends N02 to the guest (`{orderRef, pickupDate, pickupPlaceId}`, in `order.language`) and N03 to staff (`{orderRef}`, in the staff/UI language, per T0's convention) through `sendMail`

### Prototype bar: "Make an item sell out"

A new group, "Review and send". The control lowers the on-hand stock of the first cart gift's option to the units already held, so nothing is left to request. It is disabled when the cart is empty. Reset restores the stock.
