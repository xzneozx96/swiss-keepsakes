# M19 Advice requests and questions

15 October 2026. Step 4.14 is built and awaiting the owner's batch review.

Built directly in the React app with evon ("just build it"), matching M03 (tabs with counts, phone cards, desktop table) and M04 (detail cards, back link, two columns from 1024px).

## Review the changes

Switch to **Merchant · Owner** (or Store Manager, D12) and open Requests (`#/store/requests`, under More on phones). Try it in EN and DE.

- **One page, three tabs.** The tabs are Advice, Private tours (step 4.15) and Questions, in the M03 style with counts. The selected tab is kept in `?tab=` (`questions`, `private`; no value means Advice). Each count is the number of items in that tab, as on M03.
- **Advice inbox.** Requests are listed newest first.
  - **Phone card** (M03 card frame): the guest's email, then **New** or **Read**, the guest's language and the time sent. Under that are the first three answers, each under its question in the wording the guest saw (`lang` set per question), then "+4 more answers". The whole card opens the request.
  - **Table from 1024px:** Guest (email and language), Answers (the first two answers and the count), Sent, Status. A row opens the request.
- **Request detail** (`#/store/requests/advice/{id}`, M04 frame):
  - **Header:** back link "Requests", the h1 "Advice request", then the email, the time and the language the request was sent in.
  - **"Questions and answers" card:** every question with the wording as sent and its answer. The line under the title explains that later changes to the questions don't change this request. A question shown in another language than the guest's carries a tag, "Asked in English" (D7). A closing line notes that skipped questions are not listed.
  - **"Guest" card:** the email with Copy (M04's `CopyText`), the language, and "Reply by email". The reply link is a `mailto:` with a subject in the guest's language. Replies happen outside the app (D9).
  - Opening the detail marks the request read, once. The inbox then shows "Read".
  - **Old wording (AC18).** The seeded older request (h.whitfield) still shows "How many children (under 16)?" and "What are you interested in?", although the current set words those questions differently.
- **Questions tab (the question set).**
  - **Note at the top:** changes apply to new requests only, sent requests keep their wording, and a guest who has already started keeps the questions they began with (AC18).
  - **"Where requests go":** the recipient email (N16 goes there, step 4.17) with its own Save (ink, enabled once the address changes). A malformed address shows "Enter a valid email address." and is not saved.
  - **"Questions" card:** "Add question", then "Asked in this order · last changed …".
    - **Translation warning:** "1 question is missing a translation. Guests then see the other language." The count covers a question's own text and its answer options.
    - **Each row:** its number, the EN and DE text (a dash where one is missing), then the answer type, Required or Optional, and the option count. A missing language adds a "DE missing" or "EN missing" chip. Up and down arrows reorder; the first row cannot move up and the last cannot move down.
    - A tap on a row opens its sheet.
  - **Question sheet** (the M08/M15 sheet frame):
    - **Text:** "Question in English" and "Question in German". When one is empty and the other filled, a warning names who sees the other language.
    - **Answer type:** Number, Single choice, Multiple choice, Date or Short text.
    - **Answer options** for choice types: each option has EN and DE fields, a remove button, and "DE missing" when one language is empty. "Add option" adds another.
    - **"Guests must answer"** switch (optional questions offer "Skip").
    - **Buttons:** "Save question" (the screen's one red action), "Go back", and for an existing question "Delete question" through the shared confirmation dialog.
- **Outline step 15, end to end.** All of it is covered by the capture:
  - Add the missing DE text to "Anything else we should know?": the warning and the chip clear.
  - Move "How do you like to travel?" up.
  - Make the children question optional.
  - Change the recipient.
  - The next C21 request uses the new set, in the new order, with the new question, without the deleted one and with the changed type. The open conversation keeps its own set (side path).

## Decisions to confirm

- **Save per change, no set-level draft.** Each sheet saves one question, the arrows move at once, and the recipient has its own Save. Every change stamps "last changed". A refresh keeps what was saved and drops an open sheet (outline §1 Refresh). There is no "publish the set" step: each change applies to new requests at once.
- **EN and DE side by side in the sheet, not tabs.** A question is one short sentence, and seeing both fields makes the missing one obvious. M12/M13 use tabs for long texts.
- **Validation:**
  - A question needs text in at least one language.
  - A choice question needs at least 2 options, each named in at least one language.
  - A missing language is a warning, never a block (D7, AC30).
- **Type changes:**
  - Changing away from a choice type drops the options.
  - Changing away from Number drops the lowest value. Adults keep their minimum of 1; the sheet does not expose the minimum.
  - New questions start as optional Short text with fresh ids.
- **The last question cannot be deleted.** The sheet says "The trip assistant needs at least one question."
- **Change in C21: optional counts** (`advice-chat.tsx`). Before, an optional number question offered only "Skip" until the counter was touched, so a guest could not send the shown value (for example 0 children). It now offers **Send** (with the shown value) and **Skip**. This case only exists once M19 makes a number optional. Nothing else in C21 changed; 4.5-capture still passes unchanged.
- **The recipient is a shop setting, not part of the question snapshot.** N16 goes to the address saved when the guest sends, even for a conversation started before the change.
- **Tab counts are all items (as M03), not only new ones.** New requests stand out by the New chip and sit at the top while they are the newest.
- **The inbox shows the first answers, the detail shows all.** The brief asks for each question with the wording as sent. A phone card shows the first three answers under their questions plus "+n more answers", and the table shows the first two plus the count. Every question and answer is on the detail page, so the inbox stays scannable with seven or more questions per request.
- **The Questions tab has no red primary.** "Add question" is the M15-style secondary button; the recipient "Save" is ink (tertiary), so red is used once at most. In the sheet, "Save question" is the one red action.

## Note open items

- **Toasts pause while the pointer is over them.** After "Save question" the mouse pointer rests where the toast appears, so with a mouse the toast stays until the pointer moves. In the first delete screenshot it covered the confirm button. [4.14-toast-probe.mjs](4.14-toast-probe.mjs) ([output](4.14-toast-probe.out)) shows the toast closing after 4 seconds once the pointer has moved, and also while another sheet is open. This is React Aria's pause-on-hover in the shared `AppToasts`, not M19 code; touch phones don't hover. The capture closes toasts before the delete screenshot.
- **M02 Overview** does not count new advice or private-tour requests. Nothing in the spec asks for it in Phase 4.
- **Phase 4 coverage:** the merchant cannot reply from the app or forward a request (D9, outline §6).
