- **List by language.** M15's filter chips with counts: All 9, English 4, German 5. The language is kept in `?lang=`. Each language version is its own card in M03's ticket shape:
  - the cover photo, the title, then language · author · read time

    - "No German version" / "No English version" when the story lacks the other language
    - a warning "1 linked item is unpublished" when a linked gift or tour no longer shows
  - under the dashed line: the last change ("Published 01.10.2026" or "Edited 11.10.2026"), links ("2 of 6") and the comment count

  From 1024px the list is a table. A language with no articles shows "No articles in German yet." The newest change comes first.
- **Editor (`#/store/content/articles/{id}`, `new` for a new one), in M04's frame.**
  - The back link "All articles", the title and the status chip.
  - **Publication panel (M04 action panel).** "Draft: guests can't see it" or "Published in English" and since when, an "Unsaved changes" line, the error summary, and the actions:
    - a draft has **Publish** (red) and **Save draft**
    - a published article has **Save changes** (red, active once something changed) and **Unpublish**
  - **Details.** The language (new articles only), title, short summary, lead paragraph, author (name and role, prefilled with the signed-in staff member) and cover photo.
  - **Sections.** Each section is a card with its number, move up and down, remove, and the heading (a stop in C20's contents bar). Its blocks come in reading order, each with move and remove, and "Add block" offers four types:
    - paragraph
    - photo with caption
    - YouTube video (link or ID, title, optional caption)
    - audio (recording, title, optional caption)
  - **Gifts and tours (R16, AC31).** The links in the shop's order, "2 of 6", with move and remove, from a sheet of published gifts and tours only.
    - At 6 the add buttons turn off and a note says to remove one first.
    - A link whose item was unpublished later stays listed with the warning "Unpublished: hidden on the story"; C20 hides it.
    - A sold-out gift says so, as C20 shows it sold out.
  - **More to discover (R18, AC33).** Up to 3 published stories in the same language, never the story itself, in order. With none chosen it says the newest other stories show.
  - **Other language.** Open the other version with its status, or "Add German version", which creates a draft copy to translate. This covers the side path "DE version missing".
  - From 1024px the details and sections sit on the left, and the panel, links, stories and language card in a 320px column. On phones the panel comes first and the actions repeat at the end.
- **Validation.** A draft needs only a title. Publishing needs everything C20 shows:
  - the title, summary, lead, author name and role, and cover
  - at least one section, each with a heading and a block
  - each block's content, photo captions (they are the photos' alt text), and video and recording titles (they name the play buttons)

  Errors show under each field, the panel counts them, and focus moves to the first one. A malformed YouTube link is an error even for a draft. A pasted link shows the ID it found.
- **Media.** In the prototype, photos and recordings come from the shop's library (the bundled files) in a sheet, instead of an upload, and the sheet says so. Only recordings exist; no AI audio.
- **Publishing.** The article shows on C19 in its language only, and its links appear on C20 in the shop's order. Unpublishing asks first, with the shared dialog, and takes it off C19.
- **Leaving without saving.** Edits stay in the editor until a save. A refresh keeps the saved article and drops unsaved edits (outline side path "Refresh").

## Decisions to confirm

- **Filter default.** The language chips start on All, so both versions and the DE draft are visible at once. Choosing a language narrows the list.
- **New article.** `new` opens an empty draft that is stored on its first save. The URL then becomes the article's id, so a refresh or Back never creates empty drafts. The language is chosen in the editor and is fixed after that.
- **Other language version.** "Add German version" copies this version's text, sections, links and picks into a German draft with the same slug. The author's role is translated when it is a known staff role. The merchant translates it before publishing.
- **Actions.** One red action at a time: Publish for a draft, Save changes for a published article. The actions sit in the publication panel and repeat at the end on phones. There is no sticky bar, because the merchant shell's bottom navigation is already fixed there.
- **Confirmations.**
  - Unpublish asks with the shared dialog.
  - Removing a section, or a block that has content, asks too (owner rule 13.10.2026).
  - Removing a link or a picked story does not ask: nothing written is lost, it is undone by adding it again, and nothing changes until a save.
- **Unpublished links** stay in the list with a warning and still count toward the 6, the same way C20 counts before it filters.
- **Read time** is computed on save: 200 words a minute, rounded up, plus one minute per video or recording. A seeded value can change after a save. The Pilatus draft goes from the seeded 4 minutes to 1.
- **Publishing again** sets a new publication date, so a republished story moves to the top of C19.
- **Author name.** New articles, comment replies and review replies by the Owner show "Ngọc", as the seeded stories and shop comments do. The seeded gift review reply (r1) uses "Chị Ngọc" from the catalog seed. Pick one public name for the Owner.

## Note open items

- **Unpublishing a gift in M12** (outline step 12, last sentence) belongs to the products track. The capture sets `published: false` in demo data instead. Step 4.18 can drive it through M12.
- **Leaving with unsaved edits** gives no warning. A refresh or Back drops them silently, as the outline's refresh rule allows.
- **No delete for articles.** The spec lists drafts, publishing and unpublishing only.
- **Uploads.** Photos and recordings come from the bundled library. A real upload needs storage, which the prototype doesn't have.
- **Dates.** M17 uses the app's shared `formatDate`, in the browser's time zone, like C20.
