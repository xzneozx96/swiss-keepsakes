# Step 1.5 report: C04 Cart

## 1. What I built

C04 Cart at `#/cart`. It replaces the placeholder. The tab bar is hidden, following the detail-screen pattern, and the screen has a fixed bottom action bar.

- **Orders (R12, AC28).** Cart lines are grouped into "Order 1", "Order 2", and so on. A group shares one payment policy (kind plus its numbers), one pickup date and one pickup place. Each order card shows:
  - its title
  - the policy sentence: "Full payment after confirmation", "30% deposit after confirmation" or "Pay later, after pickup"
  - "Requested pickup Wed 21 Oct · Shop Altstadt"
  - its lines
  - its subtotal
- **Line.** Each line shows the photo, name, option (left out for one-option gifts such as "Standard"), engraving ("Engraving “Anna”"), the line total, a "Remove" link with an aria-label naming the gift, and the shared Airbnb-style `QuantityStepper`. The quantity is capped at the units available (`availableUnits`), as on C03.
- **Top of screen.** Below the header: a summary ("2 gifts · 2 orders") and a note that dates are requests and each order is confirmed and paid on its own.
- **Bottom bar.** "Total for reference" CHF 143.00, the "Nothing to pay now" chip, and **Continue**.
- **States:**
  - **Skeleton**: `useSimulatedLoading`.
  - **Empty**: one sentence ("Your cart is empty.") and one action ("Browse gifts" → `/gifts`).
  - **Invalid date (AC04)**: the line keeps its place in its order and gets a warning Alert, for example "Thu 15 Oct can no longer be requested. Needs 3 working days to engrave. The earliest pickup is Fri 16 Oct." The reason wording is the same as the C03 calendar (`detail.prep.*`, `detail.legend.closed`). The button "Request Fri 16 Oct instead" moves the line to the earliest valid date. If that date matches an identical line, the two merge. A moved line can also join or leave an order.
- **Continue.** `CartView` takes an `onContinue` prop. The route currently passes a no-op, with a comment saying step 1.7 wires it (sign-in sheet, then C06). While a line has an invalid date, Continue does not call `onContinue`. Instead the bar shows "Choose a new date for 1 gift first." and focus moves to the line's fix button, as on C03.

What `groupCartIntoOrders` returns for each order:
- `paymentPolicy`
- `pickupDate`
- `pickupPlaceId`
- `subtotal` in Rappen
- `lines`: each with `product`, `variant`, `unitPrice`, `total` and an optional `invalidDate {earliest, reason: "too-early" | "closed"}`

Prices are read from the catalogue at call time; step 1.7 snapshots them (AC15). C06 should refuse to send while any line has `invalidDate`.
