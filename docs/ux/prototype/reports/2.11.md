## 1. What was there and what changed

Codex's `TourManifestPage` listed bookings with group, payment chips and attendance buttons, behind a confirmation dialog. Gaps:

- The booking reference links used raw `/store/...` hrefs and broke the hash router (see 2.10). Fixed with TanStack `Link`.
- Contact details showed the name only. The phone is now a `tel:` link on cards and table rows.
- The group read "2 adults · 1 children". It now reads label first: "Adults 2 · Children 1 · Infants 0", so no plural forms are needed.
- No notice explained when results open, or that a departure is never completed by time alone. The header now shows the phase chip (Open, Ended, Cancelled), the seat summary and the balance to collect. Below it sits one line:
  - **Ended:** "Results to record: n … Nothing is completed automatically."
  - **Upcoming:** "Results can be recorded after the tour ends at 12:00."
  - **Cancelled:** "You cancelled this departure on …" (not a no-show).
- The buttons read "Mark attended". They now read "Service delivered" and "No-show". Each has its own confirmation copy. The no-show copy says money paid and the balance stay, with no completion, commission or review.
- A delivered booking with debt now shows the locked Complete pattern from M04 (decision 294): a disabled button, "Complete is locked:", and a reason chip such as "CHF 156.10 still owed".
- Disabled result buttons were faint red. They now use the tertiary style while disabled.
- The order is now active bookings first, expired holds last. Seeded bookings without history show an undated note instead of "on .".

Files (mine): `features/tours/merchant/manifest-page.tsx`, `attendance-actions.tsx`, `shared.tsx`, `messages.ts`. Results go through `outcome()` in `use-tours.ts` (mine), which calls the pure `recordOutcome()` in `merchant/lib/transitions.ts`. The guard runs on the screen and again inside `update`.
