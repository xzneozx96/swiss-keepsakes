# M11 Products

15 October 2026. Step 4.8 is built and awaiting the owner's batch review.

## Review the changes

Switch the prototype bar to **Merchant · Owner** and open Products (`#/store/products`). Try it in EN and DE, at 375px and from 1024px. Manager sees the same screen (D12).

- **M03's page shape.** The title and one line of intro ("Text in English or German is enough to publish"), then three tabs with counts, as M03's status tabs: **Gifts**, **Tours** and **Pickup points** (D3). The selected tab is kept in the address (`?tab=tours`, `?tab=pickup`; Gifts has none), so a reload or a back link keeps it. On phones the tab list scrolls sideways and the selected tab is scrolled into view, as on M03.
- **Gift and tour tickets (phones).** One white card per gift or tour in the M03 ticket grammar:
  - photo, name and a meta line ("Pocket knives · 3 options", or "2 h" for a tour), and a chevron;
  - two chips: **Published** or **Draft**, then the language state: **EN and DE**, **DE missing**, **EN missing** or **Text missing**;
  - a dashed footer with one fact per row: the "from" price (adult price for tours), what is available (units, or "Sold out" in warning colour; upcoming departures for tours) and the commission.
  
  The whole card opens M12 or M13.
- **From 1024px.** The same rows in M03's Table, with five columns: name with photo, status (both chips stacked as M03's labels), price, available or departures, and commission. A row opens M12 or M13.
- **Language state (R02, AC30).** It is computed from the required text each time and is never stored:
  - gifts: name, story, cancellation policy and option names;
  - tours: name, summary, description, meeting point, the stops' names and texts, inclusions, exclusions, conditions and cancellation policy.
  
  A language counts only when every one of these fields is filled in that language. The seeded examples:
  - the limited-edition print shows **Published · DE missing**;
  - "Luzern bei Nacht" (German only) shows **Published · EN missing**;
  - Pilatus shows **Draft**.
- **Empty tab.** A tab with nothing in it shows the shared EmptyState ("No gifts yet.") and a 0 count.
- **Pickup points** is the M14 tab, reported in [4.11](4.11.md).

## Decisions to confirm

- ~~**No "Add gift" or "Add tour".**~~ Superseded by [Revision 1](#revision-1-owner-15102026): the owner asked for both.
- **Language chip when complete.** A complete product shows a neutral "EN and DE" chip, so every card has the same two chips and the warning stands out. The alternative is to show only the warnings.
- **Tab style.** M03's segmented tabs with count badges, not M15's underline tabs, because the three tabs split the list as M03's tabs do.
- **Facts on the ticket.** I chose price, availability and commission because they are what the editor changes most. The order and the facts are easy to change.

## Revision 2 (owner, 15.10.2026): row menu with Edit and Archive

The owner asked for an action column on M11.

- **Row menu.** Every gift and tour row now has a ⋯ menu, styled like M15's, in a new "Actions" column on the desktop table and at the top-right of each phone card. It holds **Edit** and **Archive**; for archived items it holds **Edit** and **Restore as draft**.
- **Archiving.** Archive asks first with the shared confirmation dialog: "Guests no longer see this gift. Orders that include it stay as they are." It sets `archivedAt` and `published: false`, and a toast confirms.
- **Where archived items go.** An archived gift or tour leaves the "Active" list and appears under the new **Active / Archived** filter with an "Archived" chip. Guests don't see it anywhere: `isPublished()` returns false for archived gifts, and C08 filters out archived tours.
- **Restoring.** "Restore as draft" brings an item back as a draft.
- **Links.** Table rows are no longer links, because a press inside the menu also opened the row. The name links to the editor, as on M15. On phones the whole card still opens the editor, through a stretched link that stays under the menu.
- **Check.** [4.8-r2-archive-check.mjs](4.8-r2-archive-check.mjs) passes 20 of 20 at 1280px and 375px. It covers the menu items, archive with confirmation, staying on M11, the data, the Archived filter, C02 hiding the gift, restore as draft, Edit opening M12, and no console errors.
- **Shared files touched:** `catalog/types.ts` and `tours/types.ts` (`archivedAt`), `catalog/lib/publish.ts`, `tours/discovery/tour-list-page.tsx`, `components/icons.ts` (`EditIcon`, `RestoreIcon`).
