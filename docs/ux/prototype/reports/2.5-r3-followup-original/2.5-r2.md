### What changed

All app changes are in `features/tours/booking/`.

- **`tour-review-page.tsx`, the footer unit.** The signed-out `section` moved out of the content column into the sticky footer, above the button. It keeps its `aria-labelledby` heading.
  - The heading is now the serif card-title size: 18px, down from the 24px `text-title`. The description is `text-sm` muted. A 24px title would take too much of a 812px phone screen inside a sticky bar.
  - The block sits 12px (`mb-3`) above the button. That is the gap C06 leaves between its total row and its button (`gap-3`).
  - The footer `div` and its classes are unchanged. Its padding was already C06's: `px-6`, `pt-3`, `pb-[calc(env(safe-area-inset-bottom,0px)+12px)]`. When signed in, the footer DOM is the same as before.
- **`tour-review-page.tsx`, keyboard.** The taller footer could hide a focused field. `scroll-mb-40` now sits on C11's own inputs and on the two alert buttons, so Tab scrolls each one clear of the footer.
  - This is a scroll margin only, so no pixel changes (see the compare).
  - It also fixes a pre-existing case on signed-in C11: Tab left the child's-name field under the 69px bar.
  - I did not add a global `scroll-padding`, which would change gift pages.
- **`messages.ts`, `tourBooking.signIn.body`.** Only the values of this key changed:
  - EN: "Your booking is saved to your account, and your details stay filled in."
  - DE: "Ihre Buchung wird in Ihrem Konto gespeichert und Ihre Angaben bleiben ausgefüllt."
