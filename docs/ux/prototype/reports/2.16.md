- **Rebuild:**
  ```sh
  node docs/ux/prototype/reports/2.16-manifest.mjs
  cd docs/ux/prototype/screens/phase2-review && node build-review.mjs
  ```
  Verdicts are keyed by build time, as in Phase 1, so **a rebuild starts the owner's review over**. Rebuild only before the owner begins.

**How the owner reviews (same as Phase 1):**
- Per item: Approved, or Not approved with a required comment. Clicking the active toggle again clears it.
- A comment can also be added without a verdict.
- The sticky bar shows counts and filters (All, Not reviewed, Approved, Not approved), plus **Copy as text** and **Download review**, which saves `phase2-review.json` as `{reviewedAt, items:[{item, title, verdict, comment}]}`.
- Export is blocked while a Not approved item has no comment, and the notice names that item.
- Verdicts are saved in the browser's localStorage. **Reset verdicts** at the bottom asks first.
- Thumbnails open a viewer that steps through one item's shots, with arrows or Prev/Next. Esc closes it.
