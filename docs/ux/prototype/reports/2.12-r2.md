### M07 (`features/tours/merchant/booking-page.tsx`), toward M04 order detail (1.11r, 1.11s, 1.15r)

- **Header.** The M04 `OrderHeader` look:
  - an icon back link to the guest list;
  - the booking reference as `h1`, with the tour name and start under it;
  - the booking and payment chips right-aligned from 1024px.
- **Key facts as standalone cards (1.11s).** The money panel's own facts are four `StatCard`s in M04's gapped grid: 2 across on phones, 4 from 1024px, no vertical dividers.
  - The facts are Booking total, Received, Outstanding balance and Balance due.
  - A cancelled or expired booking shows only Booking total and Received, as before.
  - No new fact was added; the coordinator approved this.
- **Money panel.** Still the M04 `ActionPanel` (toned band, "Payment" eyebrow, the money title). Its body now follows the M04 next step:
  - M04's inset money panel:
    - while money is owed: "Still owed", the amount, and "Paid X of Y";
    - once settled or closed: "Received" and the amount.
  - The overdue state (2.14).
  - Notes with M04's icon-note style: cancelled money (info icon), reconciliation (danger icon).
  - Record payment as M04's primary large button.
  - The reminder ProposalMark, unchanged.
- **Next step.** It stays a `DetailCard`, because `ActionPanel` has a fixed heading id and a second one would duplicate it. Its buttons now use M04 sizes (see 2.13-r2).
- **From 1024px, M04's two columns.** These replace one long column, which used to split only at 1280px:
  - main column: money panel, next step, price and policies, booking history;
  - 300px reference column: guest contact, departure, payment history, partner commission.
  - Phones keep the original order: money, next step, commission, contact, departure, price and policies, payments, history.
  - The money panel and next step keep their place in the React tree at every width, as M04's action panel does, so an open dialog survives a resize across 1024px. `reports/2.10-r2-keyboard.out`: `PASS M07: confirmation still open after resizing 1100 → 900`.
- **Price and policies.** The per-age-group lines and total are now M04's inset payments ledger (soft panel, total as the strong row). Before, they were rows with a rule.
- **Payment history.** M04's `divide-y` rows, with the amount in semibold.
- **Booking history.** This is now the M04 order-log timeline:
  - a 32px ring per event with an icon: booking created, payment, delivered or completed (success), no-show or cancelled (danger), expired;
  - a connector line between events;
  - the event in medium weight, then the date and actor.
  - Before, it was a plain list with a left border.

## 5. Left alone (UX change), for the owner

1. **Phone tabs.** M04 splits its sections into Details / Money / Activity tabs on phones. M07 keeps one scrolling list, because tabs would hide sections and change the flow. Not built (coordinator agreed).
2. **Step bar.** M04 shows a 5-step progress bar. Tours have no approved step model (confirmed → delivered → completed, with no-show and cancel branches). Not built (coordinator agreed).
3. **One action panel.** M04 has a single action panel that holds the money and the next step. M07 keeps two sections (Payment panel, Next step card); merging them would merge sections.
4. **Amount shown more than once.** The money inset repeats the amount from the band title and the key-fact card. M04 does the same (Balance card, "Still owed" inset, hint sentence). Should the inset be dropped on tours?
5. **No guest avatar.** M04's guest card shows an avatar. Tour contact cards do not; avatar code is off limits for this phase.
