Worker B, 13–14 October 2026. Built against the `[~]` flow-2 outline. The provider is the existing simulator. Provider results and timing are prototype behaviour only.

## 1. What was there and what changed

Codex had wired the existing `ProviderPage` to tours through `use-tour-payment.ts`, with a 15-minute `holdUntil` on creation, a countdown (`HoldNotice`), success, fail and processing, a synchronous double-tap guard, and a hold check in `pay`. Gaps:

- No tour path answered a payment left processing. `PaymentDemoControl` only handles gift orders, so a processing tour payment stayed open for good.
- After expiry, C12 said only "Payment is unavailable for this booking", without the expired state or a rebook action.
- A balance payment success also opened C13, the "Your places are confirmed" page.
- No notification emails were sent for tour payments.

Changes:

- **`use-tours.ts`, owned functions:**
  - `expireHolds` now uses `expireLapsedHolds` (pure, `transitions.ts`). It records the expiry at the hold deadline, not when a page noticed it, and closes an open payment's session.
  - New `resolvePayment` and `repeatProviderResult` cover the provider's answer for the open payment.
  - `pay` keeps its guards. It now commits through `changeAndMail`, which sends N07, N08 and N09 with worker C's `mailTourGuest` in the same update, and only when the booking's payments or confirmation actually changed (`bookingMails`, pure).
- **C12:** the blocked message names when the reservation ended. Above the provider frame, the expired notice explains the release and offers "Check availability again". A balance success returns to C16.
- **Prototype controls (Tours · guest booking):**
  - "Skip ahead 16 minutes" moves the demo clock for everything.
  - "Provider confirms this tour payment now" answers the open payment.
  - "Provider declines or closes the session, no charge" ends it without money.
  - "Provider sends its last result again" re-sends the previous answer.
