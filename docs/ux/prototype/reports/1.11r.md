# Step 1.11r report: M04 Merchant order detail, redesign

Owner verdict on 1.11: "need redesign on the entire flow, current layout look flat and boring". This step redesigns the whole M04 flow at `#/store/orders/{id}`. The decision, its sheets, every section and the T0 behaviour stay the same; the hierarchy and layout change.

## 0. How I used the evon:ui-ux skill

- **Invoked:** `Skill evon:ui-ux`. Read `SKILL.md`, `references/design-process.md` (branch U), `references/layouts/app.md` ("Đầu trang", "Trang chi tiết bản ghi"), `references/locked-rules.md`, `references/components/description-list.md` and `references/components/timeline.md`.
- **Question 1 (route):** "redesign this screen" goes to the default branch `U`: a brief, then wireframes, with two gates.
- **Question 2 (audit, run before the brief):**
  - Stack: Vite, React 19, HeroUI 3.2.6, Tailwind 4.3.
  - Project style: soft cards (`rounded-3xl bg-surface shadow-surface`, no border) on M02/M03.
  - Reused as is: `StatusChip`, the shared `ConfirmDialog`/`useConfirm`, the Drawer sheets, `Media`, HeroUI `Tabs` (the same pattern as M03).
  - Not added: no new libraries and no new icons.
- **Gate 1:** I sent the brief and plan to the coordinator with `orca orchestration ask`, options `just build it` / `wireframes`. The owner answered: "build directly … skip the wireframe gate". Per the skill's "dựng luôn" rule I built the recommended option without wireframes.
- **Question 3 (style):** I kept the project style, which is the skill's direction B (sunk blocks: no borders, separation by background and space). I made no token changes.
- **Not taken from the skill:**
  - Locked rule 16 (no focus rings) conflicts with the project rule that keyboard focus must work. The HeroUI focus rings stay.
  - The skill puts the detail-page title at `text-lg`. The project's `text-title` stays, so every merchant page keeps the same title size.
- **Self-check, the skill's five questions** (on the 375 and 1024 shots):
  1. Cards of one kind no longer change height because a line is missing: the key facts are a fixed 2×2 or 1×4 grid.
  2. Everything clickable looks like a button or a link: Copy, Write to the guest, the tabs.
  3. Nested frames: the soft inner panels sit inside the cards, one level deep only.
  4. At 1024 the columns are balanced: Policy moved to the left column after the first run, when the right column was much longer.
  5. The heaviest element on the page is the Accept button inside the action panel, which is the main job.

### Brief (U1)

- **Product:** Swiss Keepsakes back office *(read in the code)*.
- **Users:** the Owner (Chị Ngọc) and the Manager (Lukas), at the counter or in the back office, on a phone or a laptop *(spec §4.6)*.
- **Main job on a requested order:** decide (accept, change the date or place, or decline) with stock, pickup and money in view.
- **Main job after the decision:** see where the order stands and do the next step (FulfilmentActions).
- **What the decision is based on:** stock per line, the requested date and place, the amount and the payment rule, and the source (partner commission).

### Plan (U2, approved by the owner)

- **Header:** the back link, "Order SK-1044" as the one h1, the guest and the request time, and both status chips (to the right of the title from 1024px).
- **Key-facts strip:** one card with Pickup (date, place), Total (number of gifts), Balance (amount paid) and Source (Partner / No partner). It is 2×2 at 375 and a single row from 640px.
- **Action panel:** the primary action area. A tinted band in the tone of the progress chip holds the eyebrow "Your next step" and the title. The body holds the facts to check (requested pickup; stock lines with green or brick pills) and the buttons: Accept full width, with Change and Decline on one row from 640px.
- **375:** under the action panel, three HeroUI tabs.
  - Details: Gifts and Guest.
  - Payments: Payments, Partner commission and Policy.
  - Activity: the log.
- **From 1024px:** two columns, a flexible left column and a 300px right column.
  - Left: the action panel, Gifts, Activity and Policy.
  - Right: Guest, Payments and Partner commission.
- **Sheets:**
  - Accept opens with a summary card: the amount in `text-display`, "Due by …", the pickup and the number of items. The outline sentence and the stock-hold note follow.
  - Change shows "Current pickup" on top.
  - The place and decline-reason radios are choice cards with an ink frame when selected.

### Style tokens and patterns for 1.15r (FulfilmentActions restyle)

- **Card:** `DetailCard` from `detail-card.tsx`.
  - `rounded-3xl bg-surface p-4 shadow-surface lg:p-5`, no border, `gap-4`.
  - Header: a 36px icon tile (`size-9 rounded-xl bg-surface-secondary text-muted`, icon 18), then the title `text-base font-semibold`.
- **Action panel:** wrap the fulfilment steps in `ActionPanel` (exported from `detail-card.tsx`) so the next step always sits in the same place and shape.
  - Props: `tone` (pass `view.progress.tone`), `eyebrow` (`t("merchantOrder.panel.eyebrow")`), `title`, `icon`, `headingRef`.
  - `headingRef` is meant for the existing focus hand-off. The heading has `tabIndex={-1}` and `id="action-panel-title"`.
  - Band: `bg-{tone}-soft text-{tone}-soft-foreground px-4 py-3.5`, with a white/70 icon tile, the eyebrow `text-xs font-medium uppercase tracking-wide`, and the title `text-heading`.
  - Body: `flex flex-col gap-4 p-4 lg:p-5`.
- **Facts inside a panel:** a soft panel `rounded-2xl bg-surface-secondary p-3`, an icon (18), a `text-xs text-muted` label over a `font-medium tabular-nums` value.
- **Buttons:**
  - Primary: `fullWidth size="lg" className="h-12 text-base"`.
  - Secondary actions: on one row from `sm` (`flex flex-col gap-2 sm:flex-row`), the long label `sm:flex-1`, the short one `sm:w-auto sm:px-6`.
  - Locked or blocked reasons: `Alert` (danger) or the existing StatusChip line.
- **Status pills inside panels:** `rounded-full px-2.5 py-0.5 text-sm` with `bg-success-soft text-success-soft-foreground` or `bg-danger-soft text-danger-soft-foreground`, plus a 14px status icon.
- **Money summary:**
  - Amounts as a `dl` in a soft panel; the strong row has a top divider and `text-base font-semibold`.
  - A big amount in a sheet: `text-display text-foreground tabular-nums`.
- **Sheets:** `SheetFrame` (exported from `decision-block.tsx`). Choice-card radios: `rounded-2xl border border-separator p-3 data-[selected=true]:border-foreground data-[selected=true]:ring-1 data-[selected=true]:ring-foreground`.
- **Timeline:** `ActivityCard` shows newest first, a 32px ring per event type, green only for `completed`, brick for declined, cancelled and failed payments.
