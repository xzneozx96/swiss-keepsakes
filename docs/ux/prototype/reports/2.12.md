## 1. What was there and what changed

Codex's `MerchantTourBookingPage` showed contact, departure, payment history, activity, a money panel and attendance actions. The commission line was static text. Gaps against the step:

- **Missing information.** There was no referral source, no price or policy snapshot, and no real commission eligibility. I added:
  - A "Price and policies" card: per-age-group lines from the booking snapshot, the payment rule with first amount and deadline, the overdue policy, and the cancellation policy text.
  - A "Partner commission" card: source, amount (rate and basis), and a state chip (none / estimated / recorded / not applicable) from the shared `bookingCommission()` selector.
- **Participant information.** It is shown with labels: B's `participantInfo` answers, or the older `childName` ("Child's name").
- **Order on phones.** The money panel and next step were at the bottom. They now come first in DOM order (the M04 pattern, decision 274). From 1280px they sit in the right column.
- **History order.** It is now newest first (decision 278). Activity events from B's 2.7 map to labels: `session-closed`, `payment-late-succeeded`, `payment-late-failed`, and the provider actor.
- **Closed bookings (cancelled or expired).** The panel shows "CHF x received · nothing more is owed", without a record-payment button.
- **M08 sheet.** Codex's tour sheet had no duplicate check and no received-at time. It now mirrors the gift M08 with the same `recordPayment.*` copy:
  - amount up to the balance
  - choice-card method
  - received-at time, today only and not in the future
  - optional note
  - a "Possible duplicate" warning when the same amount was recorded within 10 minutes, with "Record … again" to confirm
- **Rules.** `recordPayment()` in `use-tours.ts` (mine) now calls pure `externalPaymentBlock()` and `recordExternalPayment()` in `merchant/lib/transitions.ts`. These apply staff-only, open booking, amount ≤ balance, no future time, and the duplicate check. The rules re-run inside `update`. Each payment sends N07 to the guest.

## 6. Demo assumptions and open questions

- The duplicate window is 10 minutes, as in gift M08. A confirmed duplicate is allowed.
- A no-show booking can still receive an external payment: the money was actually received, and the debt is not waived.
- Should the guest also get N09 when a merchant-recorded payment confirms a held booking? This does not happen in the demo (merchants rarely record during a 15-minute hold).
- Payment adjustment (Owner only, spec M07) and refunds remain Phase 3.
