## 1. What was there and what changed

Codex had an `outcome()` action (delivered, no-show, completed), a completed-only review button (`features/tours/reviews/tour-booking-review.tsx`) and no tour data in P02. Changes:

- **Completion.** `recordOutcome()` (pure, in `merchant/lib/transitions.ts`) completes only when the booking is delivered and the balance is 0. It sets `commissionRecordedAt` once (`existing ?? now`), logs who and when, and sends N15 to the guest in the same `update`. A repeat finds the booking already completed and changes nothing.
  - Delivered and no-show need a confirmed booking whose tour has ended.
  - No-show leaves payments and balance untouched.
- **Commission state.** The shared selector `bookingCommission()` uses the gift states:
  - none: no partner
  - estimated: open booking
  - recorded: completed
  - not applicable: no-show, cancelled or expired
  - The amount is the booking total × the rate snapshotted at booking.
- **P02.** `partnerReport()` now adds the partner's tour bookings, filtered by booking date like orders: rows, buckets, counts, earned and estimated totals, and one "Tours" product group so the groups still add up to the headline numbers. `partnerAllTime()` counts tours too, so the all-time card agrees with the period report. Holds and expired holds never became bookings and are left out. Rows show reference, date, guest name, tour, value, status and commission only (R15).
- **C18 gating.** Codex's review component already required completed + no balance + own booking. It is unchanged except for its German copy, which now uses Sie (the coordinator's instruction).
- **Fixtures.** Added as new bookings (`tc-*`), with existing ones untouched:
  - `tc-anna-done`: Anna, lake-11, delivered, paid in full, from Hotel am See.
  - `tc-yuki-paid`: walk-14, paid in full, not delivered.
  - `tc-sofia-cancelled`: walk-19, cancelled by the merchant.

Files (mine): `features/tours/merchant/lib/transitions.ts`, `lib/report.ts`, `lib/fixtures.ts`, `booking-page.tsx`, `attendance-actions.tsx`, `messages.ts`, `features/tours/reviews/messages.ts`.
