## Review the changes

As **Merchant · Owner**, open Products → **Pickup points** (`#/store/products?tab=pickup`). Until Settings arrives in Phase 6, M14 is this tab of M11 (D3). Try it in EN and DE.

- **The tab.** One line says where guests see the text: "on the gift page, at checkout, in their orders and in emails". Then one M03 ticket per pickup point:
  - a pickup tile, the name and the address;
  - the language chip ("EN and DE" or "DE missing");
  - a dashed footer with the opening hours and the pickup instructions.
  
  From 1024px the two tickets sit side by side. Tapping the name (its hit area covers the ticket) opens the editor.
- **Edit pickup point** (an M08-frame sheet, as M15's "Add partner"):
  - the address, required, shared by both languages;
  - EN | DE tabs, as M12's, with the name, the opening hours and the pickup instructions. Under the hours: "Shown to guests as written. Pickup dates still skip Sundays at every place."
  - "Save pickup point" (the red action) and "Go back".
  
  Saving closes the sheet and confirms with a toast ("Pickup point Bahnhof saved").
- **Rules (R02).**
  - The address can't be empty.
  - One language must stay complete (name, hours and instructions). Otherwise the save is blocked with the danger note and the marked fields, as M12's.
  - A missing German field is a warning: it saves; the ticket shows "DE missing"; guests in German see the English text.
- **Where the change shows (D4).** Pickup points live in demo data since 4.1, so the edit reaches:
  - C03's place choice (name, address, hours);
  - the matching order's C15 overview (hours and instructions in the guest's language);
  - M03, M04 and the mails.
  
  The demo path is outline step 11: Bahnhof's opening hours and German instructions, checked on C03 and on SK-1028 (an accepted Bahnhof order) on C15.

## Decisions to confirm

- **Edit only; no adding or removing pickup points.** C03 offers the shop's places by id, and its "Pick up in Lucerne" line names two places. Orders and carts point at place ids. Adding or deleting places belongs with Settings (M22, Phase 6).
- **A sheet, not a sub-page.** Three short fields per language fit a sheet, and the merchant stays on the tab, as with M15's "Add partner". The sheet starts on the interface language.
- **Opening hours stay free text.** C03's calendar keeps its shop-wide rule that pickup is closed on Sundays (`isPickupClosed`), whatever the hours say. Per-place opening days would need structured hours.
- **Mails show the current pickup point (open item from 4.1).** `renderMail` reads the places when a mail is opened, so a mail sent before an edit now shows the new hours and instructions. A real email is a copy taken when it is sent. I left the mail rendering unchanged (it belongs to the notifications track) and suggest one of two fixes for the coordinator:
  - copy the place's text into each mail when it is sent;
  - accept it for the prototype.
