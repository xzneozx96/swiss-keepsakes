## The figures

- **Commission after adjustments:** recorded commission in the period minus refunds recorded in it, never below 0 (D16). With a refund, the hint shows both: "CHF 24.60 recorded − CHF 35.70 refund".
- **Actually paid:** payouts paid in the period, "1 payment in this period" or "No payment in this period".
- **Still to pay:** the balance after offsets at the end of the period (now, for the running one); "CHF 11.10 still to offset" when there is an overpayment.
- **All-time card:** paid and still to pay now come from the ledger (they were placeholders at CHF 0).
- Recorded commission keeps P02's order-date grouping (approved); refunds and payouts are dated when they happened.
