# 1.17r report: P02 Partner report as an analytics view

The owner's verdict on 1.17 was "ui too flat and boring, redesign entirely, use widget and charts for better visual". P02 is now an analytics page: a period control, four KPI widgets, a commission chart, a product-group bar chart, and the transaction list. The data rules of 1.17 are unchanged:

- Every number still comes from T0 `commission()`, `orderTotal()` and `lineTotal()`.
- Orders are counted by their Zurich order date.
- AC12 privacy holds.

## 1. What I built

The page is `/partner`, the same as before. In the prototype bar, choose role Partner. It keeps the search params `?period=day|week|month&date=YYYY-MM-DD`. From top to bottom:

1. **Period control.** The Day / Week / Month tabs and the ‹ period › pager. On phones they sit on two rows; from 640px they share one row.
   - The pager chevrons are now ghost icon buttons.
   - Next is still disabled once the following period would start after today.
2. **KPI widgets.** One divided block with four tiles: 2×2 on phones, 4 across from 640px. Each tile has an ink icon chip, a label, the value and a hint. A subgrid keeps the values on one line when a German label wraps.
   - **Commission after adjustments:** recorded commission. Hint: "+CHF x estimated on open orders" (or "On completed orders" when nothing is open).
   - **Actually paid:** CHF 0.00 until M16 (Phase 5).
   - **Still to pay:** earned minus paid.
   - **Orders:** the number of orders in the period. Hint: "n completed · n open", plus "· n cancelled" when there are any.
3. **Commission chart.** Stacked columns per bucket, recorded (ink) under estimated (light ink), with a legend. The y-axis has 3–4 horizontal gridlines with CHF levels; the subtitle reads "In CHF, by order date".
   - **Buckets:**
     - Month: Monday-to-Sunday weeks, cut at the month's edges. That gives 5 columns for October: 1–4, 5–11, 12–18, 19–25, 26–31.
     - Week: 7 days. Today's label reads "Today".
     - Day: 24 hours, labelled at 00, 06, 12 and 18.
   - **Future buckets:** not drawn and not focusable. Their axis label stays, so the period still reads as complete. The running bucket's label is bold.
   - **Readout:** a fixed box above the plot shows one bucket: its name, order count, recorded and estimated amounts. It opens on the latest bucket with orders. Hover, or keyboard focus on a column (each column is a focusable button with a full aria-label), switches it. There is no floating tooltip. Column caps show the bucket total when there are 7 or fewer columns. The chart remounts per period, so a hovered or focused column never carries over when you page.
4. **By product group.** One row per category, largest commission first. Each row has the name and recorded CHF, one bar (recorded and estimated segments against the largest group), and "n orders · CHF value" plus "+CHF x estimated".
5. **Transactions** with a count.
   - **Phones:** one card with divided rows. Each row shows guest, ref · date, gifts and value, then the status chip and the commission amount with its chip.
   - **From 1024px:** a three-column Table: Order (guest, ref · date, gifts, status chip), Value, Commission. Nothing wraps inside the 768px frame.
   - Rows still open nothing.

**States, all still reachable:**
- the skeleton, redrawn to the new block heights
- month (default), week and day
- Next disabled on the current period
- the empty period, now inside a surface card, with "Show this month" only when you are not on the current period
- the URL state
- cards at 375px and the Table at 1024px
- the arrow keys between tabs

**AC12.** Buckets carry only dates, hours, counts and amounts. The rows keep exactly the 1.17 fields (checked in the selfcheck). Column aria-labels and the readout contain bucket names and amounts only.
