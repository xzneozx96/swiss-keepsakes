# Step 1.14: C12 Payment (simulated provider)

## 1. What I built

**Screen:** C12 at `#/account/orders/<orderId>/pay`, the route C15's Pay link already points to. The tab bar is hidden. The page is styled as a separate, clearly fake provider:
- a dark monospace strip "Simulated payment provider · Prototype · no real money moves"
- the system font, cool greys, square corners, and no serif, accent colour, brand name, or card or wallet marks

It shows **Pay to** (the shop, `shop.name`, marked "Seller"), **Order reference**, **Amount** (`amountDueNow`, fixed when the page opens), and three choices: **Pay CHF X**, **Fail**, **Processing**. Each choice has a one-line hint.

**States.** Each state comes from T0 `startOnlinePaymentBlock` and `payOnline` only:

| State | Trigger | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Choose | Payable order | The three choices |
| Pay | `payOnline(id, "succeeded")` | Returns to C15 with `replace`, so Back on C15 skips the provider. Knife: "Confirmed, being prepared · Paid in full · CHF 54.00". Gift box: "Deposit paid · CHF 26.70 · CHF 62.30 left, due 24.10.2026". N07 goes to the guest and N21 to staff. |
| Fail | `payOnline(id, "failed")` | Stays on C12 with "Payment declined · No money was taken" and **Try again**, which gets focus. N08 goes to the guest. Try again shows the choices again. |
| Processing | `payOnline(id, "processing")` | Back on C15: "Payment processing" and no Pay button. Reopening `/pay` shows "This payment is processing" with no choices. |
| Nothing due | Any other block (e.g. paid in full) | "Nothing to pay here" plus the T0 block text, with no choices |
| Loading | Opening the page | A skeleton in the provider's look |
| Not found / signed out | Another guest's order, or no sign-in | The same product states as C15 |

A double tap pays once: a ref guard plus disabled buttons. This mattered because after a deposit, T0 still allows paying the balance online.

**Late provider result.** Nothing in the UI could resolve a processing payment before this step, so I added a prototype control, **Payment provider**, with "Provider confirms the payment" and "Provider rejects the payment" for the order on screen. Each calls T0 `resolveOnlinePayment` and is enabled only while a payment is processing. The path:
1. Choose Processing on C12.
2. Run Expire (step 1.13 control). The order becomes "Cancelled · payment overdue" and N23 is sent.
3. Choose Provider confirms. The order stays cancelled and shows "Received after cancellation · awaiting reconciliation". No N07, N21 or N08 is sent.

**Files created**
- `front-end/src/routes/_guest/account_.orders.$orderId_.pay.tsx`: route, loading, not-found and signed-out states
- `front-end/src/features/payment/hooks/use-payment.ts`: the view model (step, amount, choose, retry, back)
- `front-end/src/features/payment/components/provider-page.tsx`: the provider page and its loading state
- `front-end/src/features/payment/components/payment-demo-control.tsx`: the prototype control
- `docs/ux/prototype/reports/1.14-drive.mjs` and `1.14-drive.out`: the verification script and its output

**Files changed:** `i18n/en.ts` and `de.ts`, each with one block of 21 `payment.*` keys (the same keys in both) at the end.
