# 1.5d: C04 flat cart with selection, info Alert, shared QuantityBox

## 1. What I built

The owner accepted four requests on 2026-10-13. All four are built.

1. **Info Alert.** The grey note box is now a HeroUI `Alert` that follows the design-system Alert card. It has a white surface, `shadow-surface`, a 24px radius and no border. HeroUI has no `info` status, so the Alert uses `status="default"` with `InfoIcon` (circle-i) in `Alert.Indicator` and `text-info` on the indicator and the title. The computed title colour is `rgb(31, 95, 168)`.
   - EN title: "Pickup dates are requests". Body: "Gifts with different pickup dates, places or payment rules become separate orders. The seller confirms each order before you pay, and you pay each one on its own."
   - DE title: "Abholdaten sind Anfragen". The body follows the same pattern and uses "Laden", as the old note did.
2. **Flat cart.** C04 is now one list of Gift-slip cards in cart order. The 1.5c card and the agreed surface (`rounded-3xl bg-surface p-4 shadow-surface`, no border) are kept.
   - Each line now shows its own "Requested pickup {date · place}" (pickup icon) and its payment rule (wallet icon, `cart.policy.*` wording).
   - The Order 1/Order 2 headers and the per-order subtotals are removed from C04.
   - The order split appears only on C06. The AC28 mapping changes from "C04, C06" to "C06".
3. **Selection.**
   - Each card has a HeroUI `Checkbox` (24px box, the lab `CartSelectDemo` look) whose accessible label names the gift: "Select Kapellbrücke pocket knife · Medium, Wed 21 Oct". The date is in the label because two lines can be the same gift.
   - A "Select all" row at the top shows the indeterminate state when only some lines are ticked, with "3 gifts" on the right.
   - Nothing is ticked by default, including newly added lines and lines re-added by "Request again".
   - The bottom bar shows "Total for 2 selected gifts, for reference" and the total of the ticked lines only. With nothing ticked it shows "Total for reference CHF 0.00".
   - Continue never disables silently; pressing it explains (the 1.5 pattern):
     - **Nothing ticked:** the bar shows "Select the gifts you want to request first." and focus moves to Select all.
     - **A ticked line has an invalid date:** the bar shows "Choose a new date for 1 gift first." and focus moves to *that* line's fix button (`data-date-fix={line.id}`).
     - **Only an unticked line has an invalid date:** Continue is not blocked.
   - C06 groups only the ticked lines into orders. Send creates orders from them and removes only them from the cart. Unticked lines stay.
   - The selection is stored in the demo store as `CartLine.selected?: boolean`. It is additive and lives in `features/cart/types.ts`. `features/demo/types.ts` is not changed.
   - The coordinator chose **option A**: the ticks survive a reload and a Back from C06.
   - When "Request {date} instead" merges a line into an identical one, the tick carries over.
4. **QuantityBox.** The inline `NumberField` in the cart line is replaced with `<QuantityBox …/>` from `@/components/quantity-stepper`. I removed `stepButton` and the `NumberField` import. `quantity-stepper.tsx` is not edited.

**Kept:**
- remove through `useConfirm` (body now: "1 × Chocolate set (CHF 24.00) comes out of your cart.")
- the empty state and the skeleton
- "Make a cart date too early"
- the 1.4b add-to-cart toast and fly animation; that code is untouched, and the cart badge counted 1, 2, 3 on the adds
