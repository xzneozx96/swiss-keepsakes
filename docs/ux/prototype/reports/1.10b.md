# 1.10b: M03 phone order cards rebuilt as lab option L "Pickup ticket"

## 1. What I built

The M03 Gift orders list now shows each order as option L's pickup ticket at phone width (below 1024px). The ticket has these parts, top to bottom:

- **Header:** a date tile (weekday, day, month) separated by a rule, then the guest name, reference, pickup icon and place, and a chevron.
- **Gifts:** every order line with a 56px photo, its name, its variant and engraving (when present) and the quantity "×n".
- **Progress:** a dot followed by the progress text, both in the tone colour.
- **Footer:** a dashed rule, then "Order total" with the amount, then a list with "Payment" and the short status, followed by one row for each amount or date (Paid, Balance, Amount, Due date). The payment rows are tinted only for warning or danger.

The whole ticket is one `<a href="#/store/orders/<id>">` with `focus-visible:focus-ring`. Classes, spacing and type sizes are copied from `OrderTickets` in the lab.

These parts are unchanged: the tabs, counts, the `?status` param, the empty state, the skeleton and the 1024px Table. The lab has no desktop variant of L.

Files changed (all inside my ownership):
- `front-end/src/features/merchant/orders/components/order-rows.tsx`: rebuilt `StackedRows`, added fields to `OrderRowProps`. `OrderTable` is unchanged.
- `front-end/src/features/merchant/orders/hooks/use-merchant-orders.ts`: each row now also has `giftLines` (product photo and variant from `products`; the variant is hidden for single-variant products; engraving uses `cart.line.engraving`), `total` (`orderTotal`), `paymentStatus`, `paymentFacts` (built from `paymentLabel(...).vars`) and `pickupDay`.
- `front-end/src/i18n/en.ts` and `de.ts`: a new `merchantOrders.card.*` block placed right after `merchantOrders.empty.action`. It holds the total and payment labels, 4 fact labels and a short status for each of the 13 `order.payment.*` keys.

No lab files were edited. Nothing in `features/orders/*` changed.

M02 Today (`features/merchant/today`) does not show this order card. It renders task cards that link to M03 tabs, so nothing there needed to change.
