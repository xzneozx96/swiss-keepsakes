## 1. What was there and what I changed

**Already there (codex):** `features/tours/discovery/tour-detail-page.tsx` at `#/tours/{slug}`. It reuses the gift detail parts:
- `PhotoGallery`, `ListingDetailHeader`, the heart button, `SellerRow` and `ListingReviews`;
- highlights, a translation notice, About and reviews;
- the interactive 3D itinerary map (`features/tours/map/`) with its stop list and stop panel;
- inclusions and exclusions, age-group prices, and the "things to know" accordion;
- an empty state and a sticky "Choose a departure" bar.

**Changed (all in `features/tours/discovery/`):**

1. **Accordion gap (owner-reported bug).**
   - **Root cause.** The tour accordion used `[&_.accordion__trigger]:px-0`. Inside a Tailwind arbitrary variant, each `_` becomes a space. The selector therefore compiled to `& .accordion  trigger`, which matches nothing, so HeroUI's trigger padding stayed and opened the gap. Gift detail writes the same classes escaped (`[&_.accordion\_\_trigger]:px-0`).
   - **Fix.** The tour accordion now uses the identical class string from gift detail.
   - **Measured.** The label's left edge minus the divider's left edge is 0px on both screens: `PASS accordion labels flush with the divider, same as gift detail · tour 0px, gift 0px`. Crops: `screens/2.3/accordion-tour-en.png` and `accordion-gift-en.png`.
2. **Payment policy.**
   - **Before:** the page showed only the next departure's policy. The walk said "Pay a 30% deposit", although its 20 Oct departure takes full payment.
   - **Now:** when every bookable departure shares one policy, the page says that one sentence. Otherwise the highlight reads "Depends on the departure · shown before you book", and the Payment section lists the dates under each policy: "Wed 14 Oct · Fri 16 Oct · Fri 23 Oct: Pay a 30% deposit…" and "Tue 20 Oct: Pay in full…".
3. **Age-group prices show the tour's own age bands** from `tour.ageBands`: "Adults · Ages 16+", "Children · Ages 6–15", "Infants · Ages 0–5 · no seat needed".
4. **Language notice.**
   - **Before:** it read "shown in Deutsch" in EN and "auf English" in DE, and it did not check the summary or the itinerary.
   - **Now:** it reads "This tour's details are only available in German/English" (DE: "Die Angaben zu dieser Tour gibt es nur auf Deutsch/Englisch"), mirroring gift detail. Detection now includes the summary and every itinerary stop.
5. **No departure available.**
   - **Before:** the bottom message said only "No departures available".
   - **Now:** a sold-out tour explains "Every upcoming departure of this tour is fully booked. Explore our other tours, or ask the organiser about new dates." A tour without upcoming dates gets its own sentence. The action bar shows "Sold out" next to the disabled button.
6. **German copy uses Sie**, matching the app and the map. It mixed du and Sie on one screen.

The map code was unchanged in this step. On 14 Oct the owner had the pins redesigned: the stop list and panel became a card inside the map plus a "View more" sheet. See [`2.3-map.md`](2.3-map.md).
