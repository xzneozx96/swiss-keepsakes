# 5.2 M15 Partners

Step 5.2 of [the Phase 5 checklist](../todo-phase5.md). Skill: evon, "just build it" path (owner skips the brief and wireframe gates).

## Brief

The Owner or Manager opens M15 to see which partners they owe money to and how active each partner's link is, then opens one to pay (M16). So partners are sorted by what is still to pay, most first, and each card puts that amount where M03 puts the order total.

**Layout:** M03's approved ticket card (1.10b/c) on phones and its Table from 1024px. The side tile holds the 30-day link opens where M03 holds the pickup date. The dashed footer holds "Still to pay", plus "Still to offset" and "Report link: Revoked" when they apply. No tabs, since three partners need no filter.

## Defaults chosen

- Sorted by still to pay, most first.
- Wording is "link opens", never "scans" (S12).
- The estimated commission on open transactions is not on M15, since the spec's M15 content does not include it; M16 shows it.
- An overpayment shows as "Still to offset", never as a negative amount to pay.

## Revision 2: partner management (owner request, 14 October 2026)

The owner asked for M15 to manage partners: add and remove them, generate QR codes, and see analytics. Decisions: **D17** archive instead of delete (a true delete only with no opens and no transactions), **D18** analytics in a separate tab.

**Partners tab**
- Header: title, "Add partner", and the subtitle under them at full width.
- Search by name, code or email; filter chips with counts: All, To pay, Report link revoked, Archived. Filter and tab are in the URL (`?filter=`, `?view=analytics`).
- Card (M03 ticket shape): opens tile, name and type, still to pay, still to offset, **conversion over 30 days** (transactions per link open), chips for revoked link or archived, and an action row: **QR code** and a **More** menu (Archive, Restore, or Delete when allowed). The name is the link to M16; its hit area covers the card.
- Desktop table: Partner (with code), Link opens, Conversion, Transactions, Still to pay, and the same actions.
- **QR sheet** (M08 sheet frame): the QR, the link under it, "Download PNG" (1024px, checked: a real PNG) and "Copy link".
- **Add partner sheet** (M08 sheet frame): name, type (hotel, guide, other), email, referral code suggested from the name (accents removed, 3–12 letters or digits) and checked for duplicates. No commission field: rates stay per product (O08). After saving, the new partner's QR sheet opens.
- **Archive** (shared confirmation dialog): the QR link stops crediting new visits (`/r/$code` ignores archived partners), the report link will stop working (enforced in P01, 5.6), history stays, money owed can still be paid. **Restore** undoes it. **Delete** only appears for a partner with no opens and no transactions.

**Analytics tab** (Overview parts reused: period tabs, stat cards with delta and sparkline, Widget, tooltip)
- Periods 7 days, 30 days, this month, compared with the period before.
- KPI cards: link opens, partner transactions, conversion, commission earned (recorded, after refunds).
- "Link opens per day": stacked bars, one colour per partner.
- "Partners compared": each partner with opens, transactions, conversion and commission for the period.

**Verified at 375px (EN and DE):** no horizontal scroll; tiles fixed at 96px so names align; add-partner validation (empty fields, duplicate code MARCO); adding "Guide Léa Müller" with code LEA opened her QR sheet (`…/#/r/LEA`); her More menu offered only Delete, and deleting removed her; Guide Sophie's menu offered Archive; archiving moved her to Archived (All 2, Archived 1) with no QR button; Restore brought her back. Analytics: 306 opens (−9%), 45 transactions (+45%), 14.7% conversion, CHF 215.30 commission for 30 days.

**Bugs found and fixed while checking**
- The opens sparkline was flat: `days.map(opensOn)` passed the array index as the partner id. Fixed.
- The Analytics tab took several seconds per render and once crashed the browser: `zurichDate()` builds a new `Intl.DateTimeFormat` per call (20,000 calls = 967 ms), and the hook called it about 100,000 times. The hook now caches each timestamp's day. The owner then reported the same delay on the Overview and P02, and a separate sub-agent is fixing it at the root (`reports/perf-analytics.md`).
- Compare table too wide at 375px; now stacked blocks. German header squeezed; subtitle moved under the title. Conversion line too long in German; now the rate only.
