# Step 1.11 report: M04 Merchant order detail (decision block)

## 1. What I built

M04 at `#/store/orders/{order.id}`, for example `#/store/orders/sk-1044`. M03 rows already link there through `orderDetailHref`.

**Header**
- Back link to Gift orders.
- "Order SK-1044", then the guest and the time of the request.
- Both labels from T0, in staff wording.

**Decision block** (requested orders only, spec §4.6):
- **Requested pickup:** the date and place the guest asked for.
- **Stock:** one row per line, summed per variant, from T0's `variantAvailable` and `shortLines`. Each row reads "4 available · 1 needed". A short line shows a brick icon with "Not enough: 0 available · 1 needed". A note under the rows says stock is held only on accept.
- **Accept:** opens a bottom sheet (spec §5.5 money confirmation) with the step 9 sentence: "You confirm you can prepare 1 item for pickup on 20.10.2026 at Pickup point Bahnhof. The guest will be asked to pay CHF 26.70 within 24 hours, by 14.10.2026, 10:30." The button names the consequence: "Accept and request CHF 26.70".
  - Pay-later orders get their own sentence: no payment now, the amount and its due date. Their button reads "Accept order".
  - The deadline is computed from the demo time when the sheet opens, the same way `accept` stores it.
- **Change date or place:** a sheet with an inline calendar (from the demo today, Sundays closed), the pickup place radios and a required note.
  - Saving calls T0 `changePickup` once per changed field. The order stays requested.
  - The log line reads "Pickup place changed from Shop Altstadt to Pickup point Bahnhof", with "note · Lukas · 13.10.2026, 10:30" under it.
  - Saving with nothing changed shows "Choose a new date or place first."
- **Decline:** a sheet with the reason (out of stock / can't make the date / other) and a message to the guest. The message is required for "Other". Then the shared `useConfirm` AlertDialog ("Decline order SK-1031?", "Keep request" / "Decline order"), then T0 `decline`.
- **Stock short / last-item race:**
  - **Short when tapped:** if stock is already short when Accept is tapped, no sheet opens. A danger Alert names the line, e.g. "Not enough stock: Limited-edition print: Water Tower at dawn (30 × 40 cm) · 0 available, 1 needed. The order is still awaiting confirmation, and no payment was requested."
  - **Stock changes while the sheet is open:** the confirm button re-checks through T0 and shows the same Alert.
  - In both cases the order stays requested and no mail is sent.

**Sections:**
- **Gifts:** variant, quantity × price, engraving, line total, pickup with address and hours.
- **Guest:** name, the email as selectable text with a Copy button, and "Write to the guest" (a mailto link with the reference in the subject).
- **Order log:** every T0 log event, with the note, who and when.
- **Payments:** total, paid and balance, then each payment with its method, who recorded it, time and status. "No payments yet" when there are none.
- **Source (AC11):** the partner name or "Direct", with a line saying it is locked when the request was sent.
- **Commission:** the T0 label (Estimated, not recorded / Recorded / No partner / No commission). Each line shows basis × the rate stored at order time and the amount, then the total.
- **Policy at the time of ordering (AC15):** the payment rule from the order's own policy snapshot and the cancellation text. The EN-only print shows the "Text in English" notice in DE.

**States:**
- Skeleton on load.
- Not found: EmptyState linking back to the gift orders.
- Past orders render read-only (`m04-completed-en-375.png`).

**Layout:**
- 375px: one column.
- 1024px: two columns. Decision, gifts, guest and log on the left; payments, source, commission and policy on the right (320px).

**1.15 slot:** `FulfilmentActions` in `merchant-order-detail.tsx` renders in place of the decision block once the order is past `requested`. It returns null for now. Step 1.15 fills it with Ready, Handed over, Complete and Record payment.

**Files created:**
- `front-end/src/routes/store/orders/$orderId.tsx`
- `front-end/src/features/merchant/order-detail/hooks/use-merchant-order-detail.ts`
- `front-end/src/features/merchant/order-detail/components/merchant-order-detail.tsx`
- `front-end/src/features/merchant/order-detail/components/decision-block.tsx`
- `docs/ux/prototype/reports/1.11-drive.mjs` and `1.11-drive.out`

**Files changed:** `src/i18n/en.ts` and `src/i18n/de.ts`, one `merchantOrder.*` block each (106 keys).

All transitions go through T0 (`useOrderActions`, `useBlockText`, `useOrderSummary`, and the T0 guards and selectors). The screen has no money or label logic of its own.
