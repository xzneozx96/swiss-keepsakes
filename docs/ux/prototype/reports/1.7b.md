# 1.7b: C06 Review and send in C04's visual language

## 1. What I built

The owner asked for C06 (`#/cart/review`) to follow the cart page's style. I changed only the look and the layout. The content and behaviour are those of 1.7, with the three exceptions listed under "What changed in the content".

**Layout, top to bottom:**
- **Intro note:** a tinted info Alert (`alert--soft alert--info`, from 1.5e) with the circle-i icon and the 1.7 intro text. The language notice (R02, AC30), when shown, uses the same pattern.
- **Stock or date error:** a tinted danger Alert (`alert--soft`, status danger). It keeps `role="alert"`, still takes focus and scrolls into view, and the cart is still unchanged. Visual changes:
  - The focus wrapper is now `rounded-3xl`, so the focus ring follows the 24px Alert.
  - "Edit cart" is now a tertiary button, as the design-system Alert card asks for the action in an error alert.
- **Each order, in C04's open frame (1.5c):** the header and totals sit as open text on the page background, not in a card. Orders are spaced `gap-10`.
  - **Header:** a serif title ("Order 1"), then three per-order icon rows:
    - calendar: "Request to receive on Wed 21 Oct"
    - pin: the place
    - wallet: the payment rule ("30% deposit after confirmation")
  - **Gifts:** read-only Gift-slip cards on the agreed surface (`rounded-3xl bg-surface p-4 shadow-surface`, no border; computed 24px radius, shadow, 0px border, white). Each card shows:
    - photo (72px), name, and store (shop icon + `shop.name`)
    - option and engraving as per-line icon rows (tag, pen nib), as on C04 in 1.5f
    - under a divider: the read-only quantity "1 × CHF 54.00" (muted), with the line total highlighted (`text-heading`)
    - no checkbox, stepper or remove button
  - **Totals:** under a `border-t`:
    - "Order total"
    - "Due after the seller confirms", the amount highlighted (`text-heading`)
    - the payment deadline rule, muted
    - then "Cancellation policy" and its text
  - Everything AC25 and AC28 require per order is still shown: items, requested date and place, order total, the amount due after confirmation, the deadline rule and the cancellation policy.
- **Bottom bar, sticky as on C04:**
  - row 1: "Combined total, for reference", with the amount underlined and a chevron (opens the breakdown), and the "Nothing to pay now" chip on the right;
  - row 2: the full-width "Send request · no payment yet" button.
- **Breakdown sheet** (HeroUI Drawer, same structure as C04's: handle, close button, safe-area padding):
  - "Price details"
  - per order: its title, "Due after the seller confirms: CHF 26.70" (muted) and the order total
  - a divider, then "Combined total, for reference" and the amount
  - the "Nothing to pay now" chip
  - Every number in it comes from the same drafts (`buildOrderDrafts` + T0 selectors).
- **Skeleton** in `cart_.review.tsx`: reshaped to a note block, a title bar and line-card blocks.

**Unchanged:**
- `create-orders.ts` (drafts, `sendBlock`, send)
- the sign-in gating (`requireSignIn` on Send when signed out)
- navigation to C07 (`replace`)
- the redirect to `/cart` when nothing is ticked
- all amounts, the deadline-rule wording and the cancellation text
- the demo control

### What changed in the content (all from C04, no new keys)
1. **Requested pickup is split into two rows:** "Request to receive on {date}" (`cart.line.requestDate`) and the place, as on C04. It used to be "Requested pickup Wed 21 Oct · Shop Altstadt" (`cart.order.pickup`).
   - I grepped the scripts first. "Requested pickup" appears only in `1.13-drive.mjs`, and that is C15's own string, not C06.
2. **Store name** on each gift card (the task asked for it).
3. **"Nothing to pay now" chip** in the bar and the sheet (the task asked for it).

The sheet's title, close label and the total button's accessible name reuse `cart.sheet.title`, `cart.sheet.close` and `cart.bar.details`.
