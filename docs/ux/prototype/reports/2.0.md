## Compare the layouts

| Group | A, recommended | B, alternative | Main tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Photo-led tour cards, familiar centred detail title, vertical departure choices | Compact tour rows, left-aligned detail, horizontal departure choices | A explains a small catalogue well. B reduces initial scrolling but hides later dates behind horizontal browsing. |
| Booking | Tour and group first, then money and the task | Contact or payment task first, with compact tour context | A continues the approved gift-slip hierarchy. B gets returning guests to their immediate task earlier. |
| Merchant | Departure-led queue with seats, holds, balances, and exceptions separate | Dense schedule and guest-manifest comparison | A suits daily handling of a few departures. B makes high-volume comparison faster on desktop. |

Each alternative changes composition. The brand and essential booking facts remain constant. The Impeccable principle "Exhaust the Design Space" led to two compositions rather than one imposed layout. "Prove It Works" led to checks on the rendered preview, not acceptance from worker reports.

## Keep the limits explicit

These previews use standalone HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Their controls illustrate a draft selection, provider outcome, or confirmation. They do not enforce real concurrency, process money, record service, calculate commission, or prove production role security.

The initial visual timer is a demonstration. The actual implementation must bind it to successful booking creation and authoritative capacity. M07 uses `scenario=no-show` for its no-show fixture, while the guest preview uses `state=no-show`.

Shared app typecheck, lint, and production build are not part of this design-only pass because application source is unchanged. Those checks remain in step 2.15 after implementation. The client Artifact was not updated.

Photo credits and original source paths are in [asset credits](../design-phase2/assets/CREDITS.md). Existing images are reused. No new factual merchant content or tour policy is approved by this design pass.
