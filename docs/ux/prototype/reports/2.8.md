# 2.8 C13 Booking confirmed

Worker B, 13–14 October 2026. Built against the `[~]` flow-2 outline.

## 1. What was there and what changed

Codex's C13 (`tour-confirmed-page.tsx`) showed the success header, separate booking and payment labels, the booking summary, the money block, the balance action and the optional trip profile. When the booking was not confirmed, it fell back to C16. Gaps:

- No Add to calendar.
- No pay-later wording about the unpaid amount and the deadline.
- No participant information beyond the counts.
- German-only tours had empty names in EN.

Changes:

- **`calendar.ts` (new):** builds a one-event iCalendar file (RFC 5545 escaping) as a data URL. C13 offers it as "Add to calendar" and downloads `<ref>.ics`.
- **Pay-later line:** "Nothing was charged. Pay CHF X by <date>." It is followed by the consequence from the booking's snapshotted `overduePolicy` (worker C's field): cancel and release, a grace period of N hours, or the organiser decides. Nothing is promised beyond the snapshot.
- **`BookingSummary`:** shows the snapshotted participant answers (`participantInfo`) and the child's name when present. Tour name and meeting point use the language fallback.
- **Emails:** confirmation N09 is sent when a booking becomes confirmed (pay later at creation, or after the first payment), together with receipt N07 for a payment (2.6).

**Design pass.** I invoked Impeccable for this guest screen and skipped its brief and wireframe gates, as the worker brief directs. This was a scoped refinement of the owner-approved layouts, so no world discovery, `init` or DESIGN.md. I read the craft floor before editing, then ran `impeccable detect --json` on the changed booking files; it returned `[]`. Reused: the secondary full-width button style (as an `<a download>`) and the existing calendar icon, placed in the existing action stack. The pay-later line sits under the confirmation copy and uses the existing text styles.
