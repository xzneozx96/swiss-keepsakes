# 1.17 report: P02 Partner report

## 1. What I built

The P02 Partner report is at `/partner`. In the prototype bar, choose role Partner (Hotel am See) to open it. The search params `?period=day|week|month&date=YYYY-MM-DD` are validated. Without them the report shows this month.

- **Period:** primary Tabs for Day, Week and Month. Previous and next buttons sit around the period label ("October 2026", "Mon 12 Oct – Sun 18 Oct", "Tue 13 Oct"). Next is disabled once the period after the current one would start after today, so you cannot page into the future. Arrow keys move between the tabs.
- **Three numbers:**
  - Commission after adjustments: recorded commission. Its hint names the estimated commission from open orders.
  - Actually paid: always CHF 0.00 until M16, which is Phase 5.
  - Still to pay: the first number minus the second.

  On phones the three numbers stack. From 1024px they sit in a row of three.
- **By product group:** one row per catalog category that has transactions, in catalog order. Each row shows the order count, the value and the recorded commission, plus "+CHF x estimated" when open orders exist.
- **Transactions:** each row shows reference, order date, guest name, products, value, the progress status chip and the commission. The commission is the amount plus a chip that reads "Estimated, not recorded", "Recorded" or "No commission".
  - On phones each transaction is a card.
  - From 1024px the transactions are a Table. The products sit under the guest name, so both chips stay on one line inside the 768px partner frame.
  - Rows open nothing.
- **Empty state:** "No transactions in this period." The one action, "Show this month", appears only when you are not already on the current period.
- **Skeleton on load:** uses `useSimulatedLoading`, as M02 does.

**AC12:** the pure function `partnerReport()` filters on `order.partnerId === partners[0].id`, the same partner PartnerShell shows. Each row is reduced to plain fields before it reaches the UI: ref, createdAt, guest **name**, product names with quantities, value, status label and commission. No email, engraving, pickup, payment or note data leaves the lib.

**Numbers:** all money comes from T0. The value is `orderTotal`/`lineTotal`. Commission comes from `commission(order)` (state, total and per-line amounts). Labels come from `progressLabel(order, "staff")`, `commissionLabel` and `formatLabel`. Cancelled and declined orders (`not-applicable`) stay in the list but add nothing to the numbers or the groups, so the groups always add up to the headline numbers.
