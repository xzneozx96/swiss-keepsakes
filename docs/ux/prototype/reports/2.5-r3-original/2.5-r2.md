- **Cancellation policy:** C06 collapses it in an accordion. C11 keeps it open, because collapsing would hide the policy before the guest books. Should C11 collapse it like C06?
- **Price breakdown:** C06's bar total opens a breakdown sheet. C11's bar holds only the action; a sheet would add an interaction.
- **C05 copy on the tour path:** opened from C11, C05 still says "No password needed. Anything in your cart stays there." That is gift copy on the tour path. C05 is the Phase 1 screen; the copy needs an owner or Phase 1 call.
- **Key figure:** "Pay now" is the serif key figure, as C06's amount due. On a pay-later departure it reads a large "CHF 0.00" (`c11-pay-later-tour-en.png`). That is true and reassuring, but prominent. Should the booking total lead when nothing is due now? I did not change it, because the emphasis rule is the owner's call. **Resolved in the fix pass:** coordinator decision D4 made pay-later "Pay now" a plain row (see Fix pass, D4).
