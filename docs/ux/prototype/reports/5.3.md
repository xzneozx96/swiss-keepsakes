# 5.3–5.5 M16 Partner detail, payout sheet, report link

Steps 5.3, 5.4 and 5.5 of [the Phase 5 checklist](../todo-phase5.md), built together because they share one screen. Skill: evon, "just build it" path.

## Brief

The Owner or Manager opens a partner to see what is owed and why, pay it, and hand the partner their QR code and links. So the page leads with the four figures that matter, then the one next step, then the evidence: the commission ledger and the transactions behind it.

**Layout:** M04's approved shape (1.11r/s): header with back link and chips, four standalone key-fact cards, the action panel, then cards. Phones: tabs (Ledger, Transactions, Links), as M04's Details, Money, Activity. From 1024px: two columns, the links card in the 300px column.

## The screen

- **Key facts:** still to pay (with "still to offset" when there is an overpayment), earned after refunds (with the estimate on open transactions), paid (number of payouts), link opens in the last 30 days.
- **Next step:** "Pay CHF 49.60" with "Record as paid"; or "Nothing to pay: CHF 11.10 still to offset"; or "All commission paid". Each says why in one sentence.
- **Commission ledger:** commission recorded, refund adjustments, payouts (method, reference, paid on, recorded by whom and when) and offsets, each with the balance after it. Rows link to the order (M04) or booking (M07).
- **Transactions:** every order and booking from the partner, newest first, with the commission amount and state: Estimated, Recorded, Partly paid, Paid, Adjusted for refund, No commission (D13).
- **Links:** "Show QR code" (the M15 QR sheet), referral link and private report link with Copy (M04 `CopyText`), email. Owner: "Revoke report link" (shared confirmation dialog), then "Issue new report link". Manager sees neither, with a one-line note (R10, Q5).
- **Payout sheet (5.4):** still to pay after offsets at the top; amount (defaults to it), method (bank transfer, cash, other), paid-on date (not in the future), reference or note. Paying more than owed shows a warning and requires a note (D6). The confirm button names the amount and partner.

## Bug found and fixed

The ledger placed every payout at noon (Zurich) on its paid-on day. A payout recorded in the morning was therefore treated as in the future and ignored until noon. A payout now counts from the earlier of noon and the moment it was recorded. Seeded payouts were recorded at 16:00, so the 5.1 figures do not change (5.1 self-check: ALL PASS).

## Second bug found in the browser and fixed

A payout just recorded did not count until up to 30 seconds later: `useDemoNow()` ticks every 30 s, the sheet stamped the payout with its own slightly later "now", and the ledger cut off entries after the page's older "now". Recorded commission, refunds and payouts are past facts by construction (the paid-on date cannot be in the future), so the ledger no longer cuts them off; `now` only limits the estimate on open transactions. Both self-checks pass after the change, and the browser showed the payout at once.
