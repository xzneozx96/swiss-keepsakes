Worker B, 13–14 October 2026. Built against the flow-2 outline, which is still `[~]` (step 2.0 awaits owner approval). Browser prototype only: the race and the capacity recheck are simulated in one browser tab.

## 1. What was there and what changed

Codex had built C11 (`features/tours/booking/tour-review-page.tsx`) with contact fields, per-age-group price lines, amount due, balance and deadline, the sign-in gate and a capacity check in `createBooking`. Gaps found by a baseline drive and code reading:

- `createBooking` returned a booking id even when its own in-update capacity recheck refused the booking, so C11 navigated to a booking that did not exist.
- C11 did not show the meeting point. The group read "2 adults · 1 children · 0 infants".
- The signed-out prompt reused the gift copy ("Sign in to see your orders … fill your cart").
- The fixed, always-visible compact bar covered the prototype strip on C11 and C16, so the presenter could not reach the language switch or prototype controls there.
- Worker A added merchant-configured `participantFields` to tours; C11 still had a hard-coded optional child-name field.
- Nothing stopped a guest from going back from C12 to C11 and holding the same departure twice.
- German-only tours rendered empty names in EN (`tour.name[language]`).

Changes, all in owned files unless listed in section 5:

- **`transitions.ts` (new, pure):** `newBooking` snapshots prices, policy, overdue policy, referral partner (`attributedPartnerId`, like gift orders) and participant answers with their labels. `addBooking` rechecks seats on the latest state. `activeHold` finds the guest's running hold. `missingParticipantInfo` checks required merchant questions.
- **`createBooking`:** uses those, adds the `held` and `participant-info` errors, snapshots `participantInfo` and `overduePolicy`, and sets `partnerId`.
- **C11:** shows the meeting point, plural-aware group, configured participant fields (required ones are required), and German/English fallback text with a language notice. The tour-specific sign-in section and the "Sign in to book" action replace the gift prompt.
- **C11 errors:** a live "not enough places" alert appears as soon as the seats are gone, with "Choose another departure". A "you already hold places" alert offers "Go to your held places". C11 leaves for C12 only after the new booking is in the data; if the recheck refused it, C11 shows the capacity error and keeps the inputs. The alert scrolls into view.
- **Header:** C11 and C16 use the gift flow's in-flow sticky header instead of the fixed compact bar.
- **Prototype control:** "Another guest takes the last seats" (`booking-demo-control.tsx`, Tours · guest booking group).
- **Copy:** EN and DE strings in `booking/messages.ts`. The DE voice moved from du to Sie, as the coordinator asked.

Routes unchanged: `/tours/$slug/book/review`.

**Design pass.** I invoked Impeccable for this guest screen and skipped its brief and wireframe gates, as the worker brief directs. This was a scoped refinement of the owner-approved layouts, so no world discovery, `init` or DESIGN.md. I read the craft floor before editing, then ran `impeccable detect --json` on the changed booking files; it returned `[]`. Reused: the gift flow's in-flow sticky header (C06/C15), tinted soft Alerts with an in-alert secondary action (as in the gift cart), HeroUI TextField, and the existing in-flow sticky action bar.
