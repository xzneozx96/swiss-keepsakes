## 1. What was there and what I changed

**Already there (codex):** `features/tours/discovery/tour-selection-page.tsx` at `#/tours/{slug}/book`. It has:
- a departure radio list with date, time, prices and a seats chip;
- adult, child and infant `QuantityStepper`s capped by free seats;
- a seat-rule note, a capacity warning and a payment summary;
- a "does not reserve seats" notice and a sticky "Review booking" button.

The selection lives in the stored tour draft, which C11 reads.

**Changed:**
- **Departures come from `upcomingDepartures`**, which also excludes merchant-cancelled departures (worker C's `walk-19`). Before, a cancelled departure would have been offered.
- **Each row adds its payment policy**: "30% deposit when booking", "Pay in full when booking" or "Pay later · no card needed". The walk mixes deposit and full-payment departures, and the guest should see that before choosing.
- **Counter hints use the tour's own age bands.** Before: fixed copy "Ages 16+ / 6–15 / 0–5" on every tour. Now the lake tour, for example, shows children "Ages 4–15".
- **Singular and plural copy.**
  - Before: "1 seats available" and "Your group uses 1 seats".
  - Now: "1 seat" / "{n} seats" (DE "1 Platz" / "{n} Plätze") through `Intl.PluralRules`.
- **The amount due today comes from the shared `firstPaymentAmount` selector.** C10, the seeds, worker B's C11 (`tour-review-page.tsx`) and booking creation (`booking/transitions.ts`) all use it. Only worker C's `merchant/lib/fixtures.ts` still computes 30% inline.
- **German copy uses Sie**: "Wann sind Sie dabei?", "Ihre Gruppe braucht 3 Plätze".
