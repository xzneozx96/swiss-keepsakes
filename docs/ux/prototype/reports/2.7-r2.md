## 5. Left alone: needs a UX or another owner's decision

1. **Held-state banner.** `HoldNotice` (the 15-minute timer) keeps its tinted, bordered alert, because it lives in approved C12's `tour-payment-page.tsx`. Coordinator: leave and flag.
2. **Long session-closed body.** On an expired booking with an open payment, the headline body holds both the release sentence and the session-closed sentence (about four sentences). Shortening or splitting them is a copy change.
3. **"Balance due by …" on held bookings.** Kept as a quiet line, not a headline. Whether to hide it during holds is open; see `2.9-r2.md` §5.5.
4. **Reconciliation tone.** It is danger here and warning on gift C15. Aligning the two would change the shared status chip on merchant screens too.
