### 2. Where the check sits

The first build put the check inset 8px from the top-right corner. Then I put DE "Nussbaum" on sale and chose it. Its name ends at 87px, but the check starts at 76px, so the check covered "um" (`placement-inset-vs-top-edge-c03-de.png`, top). A badge hanging over the corner would be clipped: the sheet body has 3px padding and `overflow-y: auto`.

The final check sits on the tile's top edge, 10px in from the right. It is 18px and reaches 6px above the tile, with its centre 3px below the outer edge. In both languages it never covers text, and the sheet body has no horizontal overflow. It works like the approved category filter's check badge (CategoryThumb): a red disc on a white ring. The inset version would also work for every current option name except a for-sale "Nussbaum".

The size and height are set by the 8px gap between tiles (C10 rows, C03 grid rows). A first top-edge version, 20px and reaching 10px above the tile, cut 2px into the tile above on every C10 departure except the first. The final check clears that tile by 2px (`check-clearance-previous-vs-final-de.png`: previous on top, final below; script [2.4-r4-fit.mjs](2.4-r4-fit.mjs)). The `placement-inset-vs-top-edge-*` images show that first top-edge version; the final one sits 4px lower and is 2px smaller.
