## 1. What was there and what changed

Codex had a working M05 (`TourDeparturesPage`) with confirmed seats, holds, free seats and capacity, plus a "Tours today" card appended to `/store`. Three problems:

- **Navigation was broken.** Every link used a raw `href="/store/bookings/..."`. The app uses hash routing, so "Open guest manifest" loaded `localhost:5233/store/bookings/departures/walk-13`. That URL rendered the guest home page. Evidence from the probe before the change: `after click URL http://localhost:5233/store/bookings/departures/walk-13`, followed by the guest home text. All in-app links now use TanStack `Link` with typed params. Table rows use `#/store/...`, as gift `orderDetailHref` does.
- **M05 lacked the step's money and work columns.** It showed no outstanding balance and no bookings needing reconciliation or attendance results. It now shows, per departure, the balance to collect plus attention chips: results to record, overdue balances, delivered but still owed, and payments to reconcile. Departures are split into Upcoming (soonest first) and Ended (newest first). A merchant-cancelled departure shows "Cancelled". Free seats show "—" when guests can no longer book.
- **The M02 tour card sat after Analytics, at the bottom of Overview.** `MerchantToday` has a new optional `tours` slot. It renders the tour card last inside the Today part, so it appears before Analytics. The card lists the open tour tasks (with counts) and the next four departures with seats and holds. It also shows "Tour balances to collect" as its own figure. The gift "Still to collect" number is unchanged, so goods and tours stay separate and nothing is counted twice (AC19).

Seat counts come from one shared selector, `departureSeats()` in `features/tours/lib/selectors.ts`. It returns confirmed, held, available and capacity, and wraps the existing `availableSeats()` that C10 uses.

Files (mine):

- `features/tours/merchant/departures-page.tsx`: M05.
- `today-queue.tsx`: M02 card.
- `shared.tsx`: formatting, activity labels, block reasons, group text.

- `index.tsx`: now re-exports only.
- `messages.ts`

Routes are unchanged: `/store/bookings` and `/store`.

At 1024px, M05's Booked, Holds and Free columns merged into one Seats column ("5 of 12 booked / Holds 0 · free 7"). This follows evon's merge-before-scroll rule. Six columns had clipped the DE attention chips.
