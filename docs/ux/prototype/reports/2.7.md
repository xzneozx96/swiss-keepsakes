# 2.7 Expiry and late-payment paths

Worker B, 13–14 October 2026. Built against the `[~]` flow-2 outline. Everything here is a simulation in the browser prototype. Real session closure, late provider notifications and reconciliation need backend and provider verification before launch (UX spec §2.2 technical requirement, AC05).

## 1. What was there and what changed

Codex expired a lapsed hold when its booking page was open. Seats were derived from status, so release happened once. C16 offered "Check availability again". The C14/C16 labels had a reconciliation wording, but no code produced that state:

- No late or repeated provider-result path, and no reconciliation record.
- No session closure, and no way to tell the actual payment time from the arrival of the result.
- An expired booking showed "Remaining balance CHF 223.00" and the label "Payment due".
- Rebook opened C10 with whatever happened to be in the draft.

Changes:

- **`transitions.ts`:**
  - `expireLapsedHolds` marks the booking expired at its deadline. It adds a `session-closed` event when a payment was still open.
  - `applyProviderResult` answers one open payment by id. If the hold had ended when the result arrives, the booking stays expired. The payment is kept with its actual payment time (`payment.at`), and `lateResults` records `paidAt` and `receivedAt` (null `paidAt` for a closed session without a charge).
  - A payment that already has a result ignores further results.
- **`use-tours.ts`:** `resolvePayment` and `repeatProviderResult`. Late money sends N22 to the guest and the staff through worker C's helpers.
- **`selectors.ts`:** `reconciliationPayments` and `reconciliationAmount`, shared with the merchant screens.
- **`expired-notice.tsx` (new):**
  - `ExpiredNotice`: release explanation, a session-closed line and "Check availability again".
  - `ReconciliationNotice`: amount, paid time and result-received time.
  - `useRebook`: prefills C10 with the old departure, group and contact. C10 and C11 show today's seats and price, and nothing is held until C11 is submitted.
- **C16 and C12:** both show these notices. On an expired booking, C16 hides Paid and Remaining balance. Payment history marks late money "After expiry" with both times. The timeline names the new events.
- **`booking-status.tsx`:** an expired booking shows "Payment session closed · awaiting provider" while a payment is open, and "No payment taken" otherwise, instead of "Payment due".
- **Prototype controls (Tours · guest booking):**
  - "Provider confirms it was paid at HH:MM, result arrives now" uses the actual payment time.
  - "Provider confirms this tour payment now".
  - "Provider declines or closes the session, no charge".
  - "Provider sends its last result again".
  - "Skip ahead 16 minutes".

**Design pass.** I invoked Impeccable for this guest screen and skipped its brief and wireframe gates, as the worker brief directs. This was a scoped refinement of the owner-approved layouts, so no world discovery, `init` or DESIGN.md. ## 6. Demo assumptions and open questions

- Who refunds or settles money that arrives after expiry, and how the guest is told, is open. The prototype shows the exception and does nothing else.
- What should happen when a payment was made inside the hold but its result arrived after expiry? The prototype keeps the booking expired, as the spec says. The owner may want a merchant action later; Phase 3 money operations.
- Session closure is modelled as an event plus a provider answer. Whether the chosen provider can cancel an open session is a launch dependency.
