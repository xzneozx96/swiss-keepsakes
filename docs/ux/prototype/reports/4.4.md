# C20 Article

14 October 2026. Step 4.4 is built and awaiting owner review. Built directly in the React app.

The owner asked mid-step for a full blog page, rich comments on every story, and links the shop configures. These became requirements R16 and R17 (confirmed 14.10.2026) in the PRD §11, UX spec §7, §4.11, §5.1, §5.2 and §8 (AC31, AC32), and the Phase 4 checklist and outline (D13).

## Review the changes

Open any story from C01, C19 or search, for example `#/stories/musegg-morning`, then switch EN and DE. A browser that already holds demo data gets the new stories and comments on its next load, without Reset.

- **Blog layout (R17).** A tall cover with the title and excerpt; the author (shop staff: Lukas or Ngọc, with role and avatar); date and read time; a lead paragraph; then sections. A contents bar of section chips sticks under the top bar while the story is read: the chip of the section being read turns ink and scrolls into view, a thin ink line shows reading progress, and tapping a chip jumps to its section.
- **Media.** Photos with captions in the body. The Musegg story embeds the YouTube video "Lucerne city wall with 9 Musegg towers": until the reader taps play, only the poster loads, and the player then comes from youtube-nocookie.com. It also plays a pre-recorded tower clock (CC0 sample from BigSoundBank, credited in `src/assets/audio/CREDITS.txt`), with an ink progress bar. No AI audio.
- **Linked gifts and tours (R16).** "Bring a piece of it home" shows the story's links in the shop's order, up to 6. An unpublished gift or tour drops out; a story without links hides the section.
- **Comments.** Every published story version has 4–7 visible comments in its own language, with shop replies and guest replies (one level, A3), laid out like the review cards. The seeded hidden comment and spam comment remain for M18.
- **Commenting (AC16).** Signed out, the bar reads "Sign in to comment" and opens C05. After sign-in, C05 steps back to the story (no extra history entry) and the comment sheet opens; a reply reopens for the same comment. An empty comment shows "Write a comment first."; a posted one appears at once (D11), is announced and gets focus.
- **Other language (D6).** A story that exists only in the other language shows that version with a notice ("This story is only available in German."). The interface stays in the guest's language; the story text, byline and headings carry the story's `lang`.
- **Trip advice.** The floating button sits above the comment bar (D1).
- **Links.** Story cards on C01 and C19, the C01 story rows and search results now open C20.
