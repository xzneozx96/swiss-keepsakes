# 5.9–5.13 M21 Reports and navigation

Steps 5.9 to 5.13 of [the Phase 5 checklist](../todo-phase5.md), built as one screen. Skill: evon, "just build it" path. Reuses the Overview's period tabs, KPI stat cards with delta and sparkline, `Widget`, tooltip and bar list, and the M08 sheet frame.

## The screen (`#/store/reports`)

- **Header:** title, one-line intro, the Overview's periods (7 days, 30 days default, this month) compared with the period before.
- **Filters sheet:** source (all, direct, each partner), goods or tours, guest group, product or tour; active filters show as removable chips and "Filters (2)". Period, tab and filters are in the URL; switching keeps the scroll position.
- **Tabs** (phones stay short, as M04 and M15):
  - **Money (5.9):** order and booking value, collected, outstanding, refunds, commission: five separate cards, each with goods and tours, delta and daily sparkline, never added into one revenue.
  - **Funnel (5.10):** visits (QR, links, direct) → viewed → started → sent → paid → completed, with the share dropped at each step; a coverage notice when tracking was partial; guest groups (four groups and "Not determined") by sales value; sources with visits, sales, completed, conversion and value. With a goods/tours, group or product filter the funnel starts at "sent", since visitors have no group or basket yet.
  - **Products (5.11):** best-selling gifts and tours; how sales ended (completed, declined or cancelled, no-shows); low stock and seats on departures in the next 14 days (ink meters); article reads and clicks; the trip-advice widget (opened, started, requests sent).
  - **Margin (5.12):** margin on gifts and on tours from sales with complete actual costs only, "Margin for 81 of 84 completed sales. 3 still miss costs and are left out.", an ink coverage meter, and the sales missing costs with a link to M24. "Not enough data" when none are complete.
- **Navigation (5.13):** Reports in the menu opens M21 (placeholder removed) and stays active on M24; the Overview's analytics header has "Open reports", which opens M21 on the same period.

## Revision 2 (owner review, 14 October 2026): gift name ran into its price

Cause: the Overview's `BarList` draws the name and the amount as two SVG text labels pinned to the left and right edges with no width limit; SVG text cannot truncate or wrap, so a long name ("Limited-edition print: Water Tower at dawn") ran into the price. The Overview only feeds it short names, so it never showed there.

Fix: a small HTML `RankedList` (`features/reports/components/ranked-list.tsx`) in P02's "By product group" shape: the name wraps to two lines at most and never reaches the amount, which stays right-aligned on the first line; the bar and the detail ("12 sold", "73% dropped off", share) sit underneath. Used for the funnel, guest groups and best sellers; the separate summary lines under those lists are gone (the detail is on each row). `BarList` is back to private in the Overview file.

Checked at 375px, EN and DE: no name overlaps its amount on the Products or Funnel tab; in DE three names wrap to two lines ("Limited-edition print: Water Tower at dawn", "Kapellbrücke Taschenmesser", "Altstadtspaziergang Luzern"), none clipped; no horizontal scroll.
