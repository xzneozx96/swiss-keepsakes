# C22 Private tour request

15 October 2026. Step 4.6 is built; approved by the owner 15.10.2026. Built directly in the React app.

## Review the changes

Open Tours (`#/tours`) and scroll below the last tour. Try it in EN and DE.

- **Entry on C08.** Below the tour list, a white card "Travelling as a group?" / "Mit einer Gruppe unterwegs?" with C07's link row "Request a private tour". It is ink, not red, so the tour cards stay the screen's focus, and it shows even when no tours are listed.
- **The form (`#/tours/private-tour`), in C11's layout.** It has a sticky header with Back and the serif title, the tinted note ("Our team plans the tour for your group and replies by email with a price. Nothing is booked or paid now."), then white cards:
  - **Your group:** the quantity row (− n +), starting at 2, from 1 to 50 people (D8).
  - **Which days suit you?:** the trip profile's From | To date pair. Each half opens the same bottom date sheet as C07. Days before today are off, and To cannot be before From. Leave To empty to request a single day.
  - **What interests you?:** optional free text (A1).
  - **Contact:** the email, required, with "We use it only to reply to this request." It is prefilled for a signed-in guest. No sign-in is asked.
  - **Send request:** the screen's one red action, in the sticky bar.
- **Errors (§5.5).** Errors show under their field and everything entered is kept:
  - Sending without a first date shows "Choose the first day that suits you." and outlines the date pair.
  - Sending without an email, or with a malformed one, shows the same messages as C21.
  - Focus moves to the first field with an error.
- **Sent (C07's layout).** The green check, "Request sent", "Our team has received your request and will reply to … with a tour plan and its price.", then "What you sent": group, dates, interests (when given) and the email. "Back to tours" is the action. The sent screen replaces the form, so Back doesn't reopen the filled form. No reply time is shown (R03), and the guest gets no confirmation email (A2).
- **Saved for M20.** Each request is saved with:
  - time, language and email
  - group size, `dateFrom` and `dateTo` (equal for a single day)
  - interests (empty when left out)
  - no `answeredAt`

  M20 lists them in step 4.15, and the N17 email comes in step 4.17.

## Decisions to confirm

- **Group size 0.** The outline's side path said a size of 0 shows an error. The counter starts at 2 and cannot go below 1 or above 50, so 0 can't be entered, which is the same choice as the C21 counter. I updated the outline's side-path row to match.
- **Single day.** Only the first date is required; an empty To saves the same day. I added this to the outline's side-path row.
